Combined titles identify longer concert entries

The 22-track Paris La Défense Arena edition includes combined titles such as “Wep / Son lari” and “Bel pawol pou an fanm / Kolé séré / Mi tchè-mwen.” Keep each slash-separated title as the single entry supplied by the catalog. A combined title is useful for finding the performance, but does not establish separate digital tracks for every named song.

Following the disc break

The first disc runs from “Lévé o ka” to the combined “Bel pawol pou an fanm / Kolé séré / Mi tchè-mwen” entry. Disc 2 begins with “Ké sa lévé” and ends with “Zouk-la sé sel médikaman nou ni.” The February 28, 2020 storefront date identifies this edition and should not be substituted for an independently verified concert date.
