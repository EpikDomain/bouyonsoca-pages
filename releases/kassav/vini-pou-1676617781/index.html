Vini Pou and Zot’ vini pou

“Zot’ vini pou” is track 7 in the 11-track Vini Pou listing. The edition opens with “Syé bwa,” followed by “Flash” and “Souf’ zouk.” Its title-related song belongs later in the sequence, after “Ayen pa mol’,” rather than at the opening.

Keeping the album entries distinct

“Es’ se an la fet,” “Rosa,” “Pale mwen dous’” and “Chouboule” form the final four entries. The table links to these appearances on Vini Pou. It does not substitute the later concert or compilation appearances of the same song titles, or assume that identical names establish an identical audio recording.
