Chiré Douvan: Best Of

Kassav' · 2016-05-27 (day precision; this storefront edition)

Explore Chiré Douvan: Best Of: its complete 33-track sequence, displayed performer credits and edition-specific listening links.

Two discs of catalog navigation

Chiré Douvan: Best Of contains 33 listed tracks across two discs. The first opens with “Zouk-la sé sel médikaman nou ni” and includes “Kolé Séré” at track 9. The second begins with “Tim-Tim Bwa Sek,” includes “Siwo” at track 13, and closes with “Pou Zot.” Disc numbers are retained so repeated track numbers remain unambiguous.

A compilation date is not a song’s first release

The linked edition is dated May 27, 2016. Use that date to identify this Best Of listing; it does not establish the original recording date of “Kolé Séré,” “Siwo” or the other selections. The table’s listening links point to each appearance on this edition rather than asserting equivalence with earlier albums or concert versions.
