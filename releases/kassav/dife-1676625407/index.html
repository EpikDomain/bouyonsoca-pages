Difé and Difé soupapé

Difé is the album title, while “Difé soupapé” is the third track in this 12-entry edition. “Sék’ sé bon” and “Pa ni pwoblèm’” precede it; “Fanm’ chatenn’” follows. Keeping the longer track title helps separate a search for that song from a search for the whole album.

The later tracks

“Ké sa lévé” appears at track 9 between “Jijman hatif” and “Trop’ filo.” “Rara” and “Silans’” close the listing. The table preserves those positions and the supplied listening URLs. The storefront displays January 1, 1995; this guide records that edition date without treating a January 1 value as independently verified release-day history.
