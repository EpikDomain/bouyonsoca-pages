The title track and its neighbours

The eight-track An-Ba-Chen’n La edition opens with its title song and “Sé-Pa-Djen-Djen.” The long title “Tout’-La-Rivyè-Ka-Déçan’-An-Lan-Mè” occupies track 3 as one entry. Its hyphens do not indicate several separately listed tracks.

Navigating the full listing

“Filé Zétwal” completes the first half, followed by “Mwen Malad Aw,” “On Chimen Pou Demen,” “Mové Jou” and “Saki La Pouw’.” Accents and apostrophes are retained in the table to help match the displayed song names. The provider gives January 1, 1985 for this edition; the guide does not turn that value into a verified historical release-day claim.
