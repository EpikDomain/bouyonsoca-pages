Sé'w nou Enmé, le concert

Kassav' · 2024-12-13 (day precision; this storefront edition)

Explore Sé'w nou Enmé, le concert: its complete 18-track sequence, displayed performer credits and edition-specific listening links.

A concert sequence with combined titles

Sé’w nou Enmé, le concert lists 18 tracks across two discs. “Intro : Zongonn + Gorée” opens the first disc, which later includes the combined “Wonderful / Si’w pa la / Mwen anvi’w” entry. The table preserves these combined titles as single listening entries instead of counting every song name as another recording.

The closing tribute and finale

Disc 2 begins with “Siyé bwa.” Near its end, “Hommage Pipo 'Sé’w nou inmé'” precedes “Zouk-la sé sel médikaman nou ni.” The title’s tribute wording is retained without inferring additional personnel or a concert date. The catalog supplies December 13, 2024 as this edition’s release date.
