Tekit Izi

Kassav' · 1993-08-27 (day precision; this storefront edition)

Explore Tekit Izi: its complete 10-track sequence, displayed performer credits and edition-specific listening links.

Where Mwen Alé appears

Tekit Izi places “Mwen Alé” at track 6, between “An nou tripe” and “Lan mori ki ni.” This provides an album listening route alongside the separate song guide. The ten-track sequence begins with “Ay mano,” “Lévé tèt’ ou” and “Fabiola.”

Two titles to keep separate

“Mwen Alé” and “Mwen viré” are separate entries at positions 6 and 9. Each has its own duration and song link; their shared first word is not a reason to combine them. “Ou chanje” closes the edition. The supplied album date is August 27, 1993, while writing and recording-session dates remain separate questions.
