Finding the title-related entry

Sonjé lists 12 tracks, with “Sonjé PSE” at track 7. The title qualifier matters: the album name alone is shorter than the supplied song title. “Ba mwen fos” immediately precedes that entry, and “Mizik fini” follows it.

Opening and closing the album

“Lakansiel” opens the edition, followed by “Pié-mwen” and “Tinayis.” The final pair is “Pa lagé mwen” and “Avè on si.” These are the displayed entries in the linked May 13, 2013 edition. No dedication, meaning of PSE or track-specific personnel is inferred from the title alone; those details need their own supporting source.
