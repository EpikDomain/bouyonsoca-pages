Kassav 30 ans Live au Stade de France

Kassav' · 2009-12-07 (day precision; this storefront edition)

Explore Kassav 30 ans Live au Stade de France: its complete 24-track sequence, displayed performer credits and edition-specific listening links.

Medleys remain single listed entries

This 24-track live edition contains named medleys, including “Medley Mazouk,” “Medley Gwoka,” “Medley Zouk Lov’” and “Medley Jacob.” Each is one catalog entry with its own duration and listening link. The medley names should not be expanded into invented standalone recording rows without a source identifying the constituent recordings.

Navigating the two-disc concert listing

Disc 1 opens with “Gadé An Ho” and ends with “Yélélé.” Disc 2 starts with “Pa Bizwen Palé” and closes with “Zouk-La Sé Sel Médikaman Nou Ni.” “Oulé” carries a Daly feature label on disc 1. These positions distinguish this Stade de France listing from the other Kassav’ concert editions in the catalog.
