The Ktoz album sequence

The 13-track Ktoz edition begins with “San Ayen” and “Abò Léwo.” “Jénérasion” is track 6, followed by “Konsa An Yé” and “Jump.” The closing three entries are “An Tet-Ou,” “Vlopé’w Adan” and “Wi.” Use the complete sequence below to locate a title without confusing the album name with one recording.

Choosing an album appearance

That display is useful for locating the album, but it does not identify the lead vocalist, songwriter or instrumental personnel on each song.
