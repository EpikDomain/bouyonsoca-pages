Majestik Zouk

Kassav' · 1989-01-01 (day precision; this storefront edition)

Explore Majestik Zouk: its complete 10-track sequence, displayed performer credits and edition-specific listening links.

A distinct credit on the opener

Majestik Zouk contains ten tracks. The opening “Sé dam’ bonjou” displays Jean-Pierre Ramirez and Kassav’ together, while the remaining nine rows display Kassav’. Keeping that opening credit visible prevents the album-level name from concealing a contributor named by the song listing.

Parenthetical titles and the finale

Two entries include parenthetical spellings: “Djoni (Johny)” at track 2 and “Doméyis (Domeus)” at track 8. “Wép” follows Djoni, and “Apré Zouk La” closes the album after “An mwe.” The parentheses are part of the supplied titles, not separate bonus recordings or alternate tracks.
