All U Need Is Zouk

Kassav' · 2007-12-03 (day precision; this storefront edition)

Explore All U Need Is Zouk: its complete 14-track sequence, displayed performer credits and edition-specific listening links.

From Doubout pikan to Pli bel flè

All U Need Is Zouk lists 14 tracks, opening with “Doubout pikan” and closing with “Pli bel flè.” “Zouk Party” is the penultimate entry. The album’s English title differs from every individual song title, so it should not be treated as the name of a missing title track.

Finding similar-looking titles

“La’w Vlé,” “Mwen lé séré’w” and “Pa Kriyé Mwen” occupy consecutive positions 7 through 9. “Bodé apiyé” follows at track 10. These full titles distinguish the album entries when searching a catalog that also contains concert performances and compilations. The December 3, 2007 date identifies the linked edition; it is not separate evidence for the creation date of every composition.
