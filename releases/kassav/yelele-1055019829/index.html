Yélélé and Zouk-la sé sel médikaman nou ni

Yélélé contains eight entries in the linked edition. The title track opens, “Tim-Tim Bwa Sek” follows, and “Zouk-la sé sel médikaman nou ni” appears at track 3. Those positions help locate the album appearance of a song also found on compilations, remix listings and concert releases.

Shared performers across the listing

The album and its track rows display Jacob Desvarieux, Kassav’ and Georges Decimus together. That shared credit is preserved rather than reduced to the catalog’s band name. The sequence ends with “Mwen di ou awa,” “Chwazi” and “Se Ou Mwen Inme.” Each song link points to this edition; no claim is made that another edition uses the same audio master.
