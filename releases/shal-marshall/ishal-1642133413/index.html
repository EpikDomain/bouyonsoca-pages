Follow the different production teams across iShal

Qobuz’s edition credits distinguish several production teams across this 15-track project. Party We Love credits Jack Spaniard Productions; Middle names StarBlu Entertainment; Motivation and Party name GBM Productions. YTM Recordz is credited for Mas Forever, One Wine and Serious Wining. Splinters and Movements both name Fryktion Muzik, while their writing credits distinguish lyricists Shertz James and Stephenson Marshall from composer Ramon McDowall. These are the retailer’s role-specific credits, not a claim that Marshall wrote or produced every performance himself. They provide a route through the album’s collaborators without inferring shared audio masters from recurring names.

Navigate the edition and its versions

The captured Apple edition begins with Party We Love and ends with the separately titled Party. Splinters is eighth, immediately before Seductive; Movements appears at fourteen. Although Splinters and Movements share credited collaborators in the accompanying Qobuz source, they remain separate songs and separate listening entries. The complete table preserves all 15 positions and the provider’s February 8, 2019 edition date. That storefront date does not establish the first release of every song collected here.
