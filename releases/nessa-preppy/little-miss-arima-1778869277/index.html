Nessa Preppy · 2024-11-11 (day precision; this storefront edition)

Find all thirteen tracks from Little Miss Arima, follow its collaborations and read the artist’s stated connection to her hometown.

Arima, heritage and the album’s release context

Nessa Preppy’s publicist presented Little Miss Arima as a tribute to the community where she grew up. In the announcement, she connected the songs with her roots and the people who raised her. The release also explains the artwork’s connection to her First Peoples heritage. It identifies Monk Music as the distributor and places the album within the label’s Soundbridge project. The announcement names M1, Lady Lava, Jahllano, Freetown Collective, Vghn and Yung Bredda among the collaborators. These statements document the artist’s stated purpose and release context, rather than supplying unverified track-by-track writing credits.

Navigate the edition and its versions

The thirteen-track edition opens with Or You Don’t and closes with Blessings. Music Is Life names M1 aka Menace and Dj Spider; Make It Clap pairs Nessa Preppy with Lady Lava. Later collaborations include Jahllano on Touch The Ground, V’ghn and Smiddy Smith on Rocket, and Freetown Collective on Sha La La. The displayed artist strings preserve each provider credit. The November 11, 2024 edition date is kept separate from this guide’s review date and from any earlier single release.
