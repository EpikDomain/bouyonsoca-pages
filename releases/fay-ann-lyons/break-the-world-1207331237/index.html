Guest appearances in this edition

The 16-track listing names Buffy on “Buff,” Bunji Garlin and Joli Rouge Sound on “Everybody Joli,” and Stonebwoy on “Block the Road.” Those guest labels belong to their individual tracks; the album-level credit remains Fay-Ann Lyons. “Break the World” itself is track 11, so searching only for the album title can lead to that song rather than the full release.

Finding a track in sequence

“Hold On Something” opens the edition and “Keep Some” closes it. “Raze” appears at track 8, between “Air Supply” and “Block the Road.” The ordered table below retains the storefront’s titles, feature labels and durations so you can identify the intended entry before following its listening link.
