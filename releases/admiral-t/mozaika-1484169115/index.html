Mozaïka

Admiral T · 2019-10-25 (day precision; this storefront edition)

Explore the eight-track Mozaïka edition, its four named guests and the ka-centred purpose described when it was released.

Ka culture, guests and a companion release

Reporting the release on October 28, 2019, the reggae radio site Party Time said Admiral T had announced that summer that he would issue two productions rather than one new album. It presented Mozaïka as foregrounding the culture of the ka, the Guadeloupean drum used in gwoka, and quoted him saying he wanted to keep helping young people take ownership of their cultural heritage, as he had with earlier titles such as Zonbi and Ti Milo, neither of which is on this edition. Party Time named Jacob Desvarieux, François Ladrezo, Sakitaw and Were Vana as guests. Looking back in a January 2025 Karibinfo interview, Admiral T described the 2019 album he called Mozeika as heritage titles that he had reworked in his own way.

Navigate the edition and its versions

The title track opens the sequence. Baimbridge cho names Were Vana, Joli kongo names Sakitaw, Chat ka tété rat names Jacob Desvarieux and La mafia names François Ladrezo, matching the four guests Party Time listed. Militè, Elwa and the closing Sèt ritm carry no featured credit. Caribbean Monster, the companion album Party Time reported alongside it, is a separate seventeen-track edition in this catalog with the same October 25, 2019 provider date. A Baimbridge cho single edition, spelled with Were-vana, carries a July 19, 2019 date; this guide does not assert that both editions hold the same recording. The interview spells the album Mozeika.
