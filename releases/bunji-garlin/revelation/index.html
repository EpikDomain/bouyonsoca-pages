About this edition.

Revelation is presented here in the 19-track digital edition listed by Apple Music with a January 15, 2002 release date. The sequence begins with Intro (Flatliner), includes two separately named interludes, and ends with Bad from Sperm. Keeping those entries in the list preserves the actual album order instead of reducing it to a selection of songs.

Flex Like This names Gauge as a featured performer, while Bump names Allure. Hands Up appears at track four; Jump Up (Hands Up Remix), at track sixteen, is a separate listed recording. The remix wording matters when locating a particular version and should remain attached to the title.

The label’s reproduced Billboard report associates Revelation with Bunji Garlin’s 2002 one-album agreement with VP Records. Apple’s page also displays a 2005 phonographic-copyright notice. These are different kinds of dates: the copyright line does not replace the storefront’s listed release date. This page documents the 19-track edition below and does not claim that every physical pressing or later digital edition has an identical tracklist.
