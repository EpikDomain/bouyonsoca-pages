Ready For the Road - EP

Bunji Garlin · 2014-04-11 (day precision; this storefront edition)

Explore Ready For the Road - EP: its complete 6-track sequence, displayed performer credits and edition-specific listening links.

Original and remix labels within one EP

The six-track EP places “Differentology (Ready for the Road)” beside a separately labeled Major Lazer Remix. It also pairs “Carnival Tabanca” with “Carnival Tabanca (Viking Remix).” These are four distinct entries in this edition, not duplicate rows to collapse. The remaining two tracks are “Truck On D Road” and “Red Light District.”

Choosing the intended version

Use the full title in the tracklist when opening a remix. The Major Lazer and Viking labels distinguish those entries from the unqualified titles, but do not provide a complete production-credit list. The edition is dated April 11, 2014 in the linked catalog; that date alone does not establish when each song first circulated.
