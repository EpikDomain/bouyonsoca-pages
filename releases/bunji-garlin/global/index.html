About this edition.

Global is represented here by Bunji Garlin’s 15-track Apple Music edition, listed with a May 22, 2007 release date. It opens with No Super Hero and closes with Hands Up. Between those endpoints are Pan and Soca, Brrrt, Fire Fi Dem and the similarly named Globally; the album title Global and the track title Globally are separate catalog entries.

Four titles in this edition explicitly name guest performers: Get Up Stand Up features T.O.K, Hardcore Loving features Rita Jones, Swing It features Chris Black, and One Family features Freddie McGregor. Those credits identify the performers named in the storefront titles; they do not supply a complete list of writers, producers or musicians.

A Billboard report reproduced by VP Records links Global to Bunji’s recording agreement with the label signed in 2006. That provides release-history context without turning this one album into a complete account of his recordings during the period. The ordered list below retains all 15 entries, their individual running times and links to the corresponding Apple Music recordings.
