About this edition.

Ayl’Ian brings 18 listed recordings together in the Bunji Garlin edition dated February 9, 2024 on Apple Music. The storefront identifies Bad Beagle in its phonographic-copyright notice. This tracklist documents that particular release, including the opening Intro and the closing At This Time, so listeners can locate individual recordings within the complete sequence.

Entering Greenzland appears near the beginning at track three, following Today. Tell Yuh Mudda Now is track six, and Carnival Contract is track fourteen. Later entries include Links, Forward and 110 Overcharge. The order is useful when finding a song heard elsewhere without mistaking a single’s release history for the date of this album edition.

Every row below links to the corresponding recording within this release and includes its displayed duration. The album date establishes how Apple lists this edition; it does not prove that all eighteen recordings were first issued on that day. Earlier singles and alternative versions need their own release evidence before they can be matched conclusively to these entries.
