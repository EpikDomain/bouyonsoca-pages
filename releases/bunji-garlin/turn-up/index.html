About this edition.

Turn Up brings together 17 tracks on the Bunji Garlin edition listed by Apple Music for September 1, 2017. It includes Big Bad Soca, Climb and the title track, alongside collaborations identified in the track titles. The complete order below makes it possible to explore the album recording by recording.

The two Take Over Town entries are different listings: track five carries the unqualified title, while track ten identifies a remix featuring R. City. They should not be collapsed into one item when choosing what to hear. Other named guests include 1st Klase on Dance In Paint, Stadic on Road Bunx, Jungle on One More Week and Damian ‘Jr. Gong’ Marley on the closing The Message.

VP Records presented the release around Carnival performances and identified Stadic Studio Productions, 1st Klase and Jus Now among its contributing producers. Those are album-level credits; the announcement does not allocate each producer to every recording. It also places Turn Up after Differentology in Bunji’s VP release history. Use the individual track links below to open the matching album recording, keeping the unqualified and remix entries distinct.
