Miss B

Nadia Batson · 2011-03-01 (day precision; this storefront edition)

Browse Miss B’s 16-track edition, its named collaborations and Nadia Batson’s account of making more room for writing and recording during the 2011 season.

A deliberate focus on the 2011 album

In a January 2011 interview with Darling Nicky, Nadia Batson said she had set other projects aside to give writing and recording for Miss B more attention. She described wanting a larger repertoire for the Carnival season. The interview also explains how Jiggle Like Dat began: hearing Ricardo Drue record on the Tin Pan Riddim led her to seek permission from Mastamind and write her own song for it.

Navigate the edition and its versions

The captured sequence opens with We Not Leaving, whose title names Patrice Roberts, and places Jiggle Like Dat seventh. Other named features include Don Iko on Party People Anthem, Tallpree on Lighters and Black Stalin on Trouble. Those feature names come from the edition’s track titles; they do not supply producer or writer credits for the whole album. Bumpers Rule closes the 16-track sequence.
