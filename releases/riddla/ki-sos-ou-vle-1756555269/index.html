Ki sos' ou vlé ?

Riddla · 2024-07-05 (day precision; this storefront edition)

Move through the twenty-two-track Ki sos’ ou vlé ? edition, its many named collaborators and the artist’s description of the album.

Riddla on the album’s personality

Writing on February 5, 2025, ahead of Riddla’s first solo concert at Tropiques Atrium, Outre-mer La 1ère’s Martinique service described Ki sos’ ou vlé ? as his latest album, released in 2024 with 22 varied titles, and said his music moves across rap, reggae, gwo ka, soca, bouyon and zouk. Riddla told the outlet that he has no single style and that the album carries his personality: seeing the positive, saying things frankly and transparently, and learning to know oneself. The concert, part of his Mi Boss la tour and marking 25 years of his career, was to feature four musicians, and he said it would mix classics with titles from the new album.

Navigate the edition and its versions

EMOJI FLAMME opens the edition and Holy Wata, with TK International and the subtitle C’est La Fete, closes it. SCK music is named on Enjoy, A la maison, Chodo Gato and SEMBLANT. Other single guests include DJ Tony Blanck on Kdo, Marcus on Reverse, Warped on Katchopine, Mister Francky on Devant, Mahalia M. on Body Contact and Sodaade on Obsession. Bénévolat appears as a radio edit credited to Riddla and Joli Rouge Sound, and Ganyé names five guests including Blue Melody School. Enjoy, SEMBLANT and LARI RÉYÈL also appear on earlier single editions in the catalog; this guide does not assert that those editions hold the same recordings.
