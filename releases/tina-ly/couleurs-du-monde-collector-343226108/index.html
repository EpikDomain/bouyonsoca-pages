Read the fifteen-track Couleurs du monde collector edition, its place-named titles and bonus tracks, and Tina Ly’s account of the project.

Tina Ly on Couleurs du Monde and its collector

In a FrekansZouk interview published on August 6, 2015, Tina Ly said her third album, Couleurs du Monde, was produced by Ali Angel and that nine singles came from it, all filmed by Nathalie Carollo of NS Events, whom she also described as a co-producer. Asked why only a collector followed, she said the team wanted a collector documenting the live tour abroad, in the Antilles and in French Guiana through a DVD of making-of and backstage footage plus the nine clips. This captured edition, whose rights line names NS Events, lists fifteen audio tracks; the interview does not say which digital edition corresponds to that DVD collector.

Navigate the edition and its versions

Pa ni tan, labelled an unreleased bonus track and credited to Tina Ly and Lynnsha, opens this edition. Twelve following titles carry place names in parentheses, from Guadeloupe and Haïti to Réunion and Portugal. A tes côtés (New-York) is credited to Tina Ly and Richard Cave, On Line (Boston) to Tina Ly and Ali Angel, and Baby Boo (Miami) names Shaalkeen Zouk Look. Je voudrais and Vini close the edition as Bonus 1 and Bonus 2. The catalog’s Section Zouk edition of Couleurs du monde opens with Intro and has no Pa ni tan. The October 5, 2009 date belongs to this edition, not to the album’s first release.
