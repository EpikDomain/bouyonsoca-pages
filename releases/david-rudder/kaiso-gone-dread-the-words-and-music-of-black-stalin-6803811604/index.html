KAISO GONE DREAD (the words and music of Black Stalin)

David Rudder · 2026-09-05 (day precision; this storefront edition)

Find David Rudder’s six-track Black Stalin tribute, with the producer’s composition and arrangement credits and a clear distinction between the album edition and concert performances.

Rudder sings Black Stalin’s compositions in Narell’s arrangements

Producer Andy Narell’s official project page describes David Rudder’s interpretations of Black Stalin’s music and credits all six compositions to Leroy Calliste. Rudder supplies lead and background vocals; Narell is credited with production, arrangements, recording and mixing, as well as keyboards, steel pans and percussion. The personnel include Theron Shaw on guitar and Tilo Bertholo on drums. Isaac Narell receives the horn-arrangement credit, and Raphael Jonin the mastering credit. This role separation matters: Rudder’s performance credit does not replace Calliste’s authorship, and the album is not presented as six original compositions by Rudder.

Navigate the edition and its versions

The six-track provider edition is dated September 5, 2026. It runs from KAISO GONE DREAD through WAIT DOROTHY WAIT and PLAY ONE, then DE SAME OLE TING and CARIBBEAN MAN, closing with BLACK MAN FEELIN’ TO PARTY. Narell’s project page separately links performances at Sound Forge in March 2024. Those concert links document a different presentation of the repertoire; they are not substituted for this album’s tracks or treated as proof that its digital release occurred in 2024. The table retains the selected edition’s ordering, spelling and listening destinations.
