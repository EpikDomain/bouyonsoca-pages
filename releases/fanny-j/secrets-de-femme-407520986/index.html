Why the two Secrets de femme dates differ here

The two supplied Secrets de femme listings each contain 20 tracks but show different edition dates: December 6, 2010 for listing407520986 and January 1, 2009 for listing973610853. Their title details also differ. The second explicitly names Million Stylez on Me and You, Krys on Bye Bye and Slaï on Je me rappelle; the first does not include those feature labels in those titles. The discrepancy is retained rather than selecting an unsupported earliest release date or declaring the recordings identical.
