About this edition.

Still Standing! is a one-track Triple Kay International release listed by Apple Music for February 1, 2018. The catalog recording runs approximately 5 minutes and 13 seconds. Its punctuation varies across sources: the storefront adds an exclamation mark, while the band’s biography and video announcement use Still Standing.

The group’s own biography connects the song to recovery following Hurricane Maria. A press release published by Dominica News Online on May 23, 2018 describes the accompanying video and credits Dwayne Skerrit as its director. That announcement date concerns the video publicity; it is separate from the February date attached to the audio edition.

The documented link between the song and post-hurricane recovery gives the single a specific place in Triple Kay’s catalog. The listening entry below identifies the audio release, while the sources distinguish the band’s account of the song from the later video announcement. Neither source provides a complete recording-session credit list, so the director’s video credit should not be read as an audio production or songwriting credit.
