About this edition.

Heat is a one-track Triple Kay International single in the Apple Music edition dated February 9, 2019. Its catalog entry runs approximately 4 minutes and 22 seconds. The link below opens that specific recording, making the release distinguishable from unrelated songs with the same short title.

The band’s own biography includes Heat in its account of 2019 and places it within the group’s bouyon repertoire. The same biography traces a longer path from early cadence-lypso, compas and zouk influences toward bouyon. That is the band’s description of its history, rather than a complete set of credits for this single. The supplied edition has no additional remix, instrumental or live track listed; those versions should not be inferred from the single’s title.
