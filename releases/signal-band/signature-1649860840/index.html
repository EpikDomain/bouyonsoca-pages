Explore Signature’s 12-track sequence, its named guests and two distinct Ani Ba Yo Love entries, with context from Discover Dominica Authority’s band biography.

A debut album within the band’s Bouyon Love approach

Discover Dominica Authority’s festival biography identifies Signature as Signal Band’s debut bouyon album, released in 2018 with Dominican and regional guests. The same biography describes the band’s Bouyon Love approach through Baby Come Back and Ani Ba Yo Love. That account supplies the group’s own musical setting for this album; the provider’s Modern Dancehall category is a separate catalog label, rather than a reason to erase its bouyon context. The festival biography makes broad chart claims without supplying a dated chart, so those claims are not used here.

Navigate the edition and its versions

The 12-track sequence contains both an Intro and an Interlude, with Easy Dancing immediately before the latter. Named guests in the titles include Shelly, Beetle J and Janae Jackson. Ani Ba Yo Love appears twice: the eighth track names Marvay, while the closing entry names Riddla and explicitly says Alternative Mix. Those are separate edition entries with separate links. Neither should be silently substituted with the Shelly-featured listening selection on the artist profile. The table preserves the credited titles without inferring writer or producer roles from a feature name.
