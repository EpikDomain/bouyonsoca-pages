Explore Rocket Fuel Riddim - EP: its complete 5-track sequence, displayed performer credits and edition-specific listening links.

A shared riddim release, with different performers

Rocket Fuel Riddim is a five-track EP credited at release level to King Bubba FM, Patrice Roberts and Problem Child. The individual rows differ: “Mechanic” is displayed under King Bubba FM with Kes named in the title; “Good Ride” is under Problem Child. Patrice Roberts is not credited as the performer of every track.

Where Patrice Roberts appears

“Some of This ‘Fair Exchange’” displays Patrice Roberts, King Bubba FM and Dwaingerous together. “Party Can’t Done” is listed under King Bubba FM, while the closing “Rocket Fuel Riddim” entry credits King Bubba FM and Dwaingerous. Following those exact row credits preserves the EP’s collaborations without making an unsupported solo-album attribution.
