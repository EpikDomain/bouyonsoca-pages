Black Beauty’s later video connects back to Impossible

A November 10, 2020 press release credited to Colton T on Dominica News Online identifies Black Beauty as a song from Impossible and announces its music video. The artist’s statement describes the song as encouraging women’s confidence and self-acceptance, with particular attention to Black women. It credits GloSho Multi Media with filming at Ti Kwen Glo in Dominica and Suggestive Inc. with editing. Those are video credits from the artist’s release; they are not converted into audio production or songwriting credits for the album.

Navigate the edition and its versions

The captured eight-track edition is dated May 15, 2019, so its catalog date remains separate from the 2020 video announcement. Expectations and Complicated open the sequence before Black Beauty at track three. Jail Life, Hunt My Own and Bomb in the City follow; Too Soon and Lose It close the edition. The artist’s announcement calls the project an EP album, while this page presents the selected provider edition and its complete eight-track sequence. No extra songs are added merely because they are mentioned elsewhere in an interview about Colton T’s career.
