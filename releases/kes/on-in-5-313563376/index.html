On In 5

Kes · 2009-03-27 (day precision; this storefront edition)

Explore On In 5: its complete 16-track sequence, displayed performer credits and edition-specific listening links.

Live entries, an excerpt and remixes

On In 5 includes “Our Prayer” and “Lion” with the explicit title qualifier “Live @ Molly Malones, La., Ca.” A separate short entry, “Excerpt from the Reef,” precedes “Come a Little Closer (Theme Song for the Reef).” Keep the excerpt and theme-song entry separate when searching: the catalog lists different durations and positions.

The closing pair

The final two entries are “Jep Sting Naina (Remix)” with Hunter and Ravi B named in the title, followed by “Right Dey (Road Mix).” The opener is displayed as “Righty Dey.” Those title spellings and version labels are retained here rather than silently rewritten or treated as proof of one shared recording.
