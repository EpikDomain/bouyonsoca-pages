Explore Wired: its complete 10-track sequence, displayed performer credits and edition-specific listening links.

Coming Over and its listed remix

“Coming Over” appears at track 5; “Coming Over (Remix)” featuring Skinny Fabulous closes the ten-track edition. Use the feature and remix qualifier to choose the intended listening link. The album also labels “Stress Away (4d Riddum)” as “Original,” preserving a version distinction within the supplied track title.

Named guests across the sequence

Other track titles name Jenna De Leon on “Island Love,” Timeka Marshall on “Tagging Along (Sax Man Riddum),” and Michelle X on “Head Bad On D Road.” These are track-specific feature labels. They do not turn the album’s displayed Kes credit into a blanket collaboration credit for every recording.
