Way Up - EP

Jus Now · 2017-02-17 (day precision; this storefront edition)

Explore the six-track Way Up edition, its named collaborators and the unresolved difference from the label’s seven-track project description.

The label’s project description and the captured edition

Feel Up Records’ own Way Up description identifies Jus Now as LAZAbeam and Sam Interface, connecting Port of Spain and Bristol. It describes horns, drums and steelpan as parts of the project’s musical palette. The label also identifies Dun D Place as a lead single and credits Tom Swindell with its video, filmed in Tokyo. Its description calls Way Up a seven-track project. The captured Apple edition contains six tracks. This guide preserves that discrepancy: the label text does not identify a seventh title, and no extra recording has been inferred.

Navigate the edition and its versions

Rupununi Rodeo opens the captured sequence, followed by Way Up with Kerwin Prescott. Chalmer John is named on She Doh Know and Sweet Lies, while Stylo G is named on Dun D Place. Number 6 is the title at position five, not an instruction to renumber the tracklist. The table ends with Sweet Lies at position six. The February 17, 2017 provider date describes this edition; it does not resolve why the label’s project description gives a different track count.
