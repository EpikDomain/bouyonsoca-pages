A shared portrait of calypso in postwar London

Honest Jon’s presents the first London Is the Place for Me collection as Trinidadian calypso in London during 1950–56. Its introduction situates the music among Caribbean migrants and musicians already working in Britain, with songs addressing everyday life, public affairs, sporting events and social satire. This is a multi-artist compilation: the label’s sequence includes Lord Beginner, Young Tiger, Lord Invader, Mighty Terror, The Lion and Timothy alongside Lord Kitchener. Reading the performer on each row is essential; the presence of the collection in Kitchener’s catalog does not make all twenty performances his.

Navigate the edition and its versions

The selected provider edition has 20 entries and is dated September 30, 2011. That date belongs to this digital product, while 1950–56 forms part of the label’s description of the collection’s historical scope. London Is the Place for Me opens the sequence; Lord Beginner’s Victory Test Match is sixth, Lord Invader’s Aguiti eighth, and Kitchener’s Sweet Jamaica closes it. The provider calls track fifteen Saxophone No. 2, while the label uses Saxophone Number 2. The table keeps the selected edition’s title and performer labels without merging recordings solely because their names resemble each other.
