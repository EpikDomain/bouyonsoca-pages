Explore Madousinay: its complete 14-track sequence, displayed performer credits and edition-specific listening links.

Shared artist credit and individual title notes

The supplied edition credits Jocelyne Béroard and Kassav’ together across the track rows. This guide preserves that shared display instead of treating discovery through Jocelyne’s catalog as evidence of a solo-only release. “Vini Séré (Madousinay)” is track 2, while “An Limiè” names Sista Tchad in its feature label.

Finding the tribute entry

Track 11 is explicitly titled “Di’y Mesi (Hommage à Edith Lefel).” The tribute wording identifies that entry within the 14-track sequence. “Atann” follows “Vié Nonm-la” near the end, and “Eti La Yo Yé” closes this edition. Matching those titles and positions helps locate the correct release listing.
