Explore Yen ki lanmou: its complete 33-track sequence, displayed performer credits and edition-specific listening links.

Shared credits across two discs

The Yen ki lanmou listing displays Jocelyne Béroard and Kassav’ together on its 33 track rows. Disc 1 has 17 entries and disc 2 has 16. This page follows that shared credit while keeping one release guide: finding the same edition through either artist’s catalog should lead to the same tracklist rather than two conflicting album descriptions.

Two An Limiè entries in the same listing

Disc 1 opens with “An Limiè (feat. Sista Tchad)” and closes with an entry titled simply “An Limiè.” The catalog supplies different durations for those two entries. Both are retained with their positions and exact listening links; the title difference does not by itself establish every performer or explain the recording relationship. Disc 2 begins with “Ti Tak Isi” and ends with “Eti La i yé.”
