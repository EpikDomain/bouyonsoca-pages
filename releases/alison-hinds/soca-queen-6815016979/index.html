Soca Queen

Alison Hinds · 2007-10-16 (day precision; this storefront edition)

Explore the 15-track Soca Queen edition, the two named Roll It Gal entries and Alison Hinds’s explanation of the album title and her move into solo performance.

Why Hinds chose the title Soca Queen

In Cheryl Nneka U. Hazell’s 2007 WE Magazine interview, Alison Hinds explained that listeners had called her Soca Queen during her Square One years, so she used that familiar title for her solo debut. She also described seeking greater flexibility and creative control after leaving the band. Her account places the album within a change in how she shaped her performances and working life.

Navigate the edition and its versions

This 15-track edition begins with The More You Get and includes Faluma, Roll It Gal and Thundah near the start. The Show appears at track 11, and Roll It Gal returns at the end with a Rishi Rich Remix label. The entry without a remix label and named remix remain separate rows and listening links. A shared song title does not establish identical recordings or transfer production credits between versions.
