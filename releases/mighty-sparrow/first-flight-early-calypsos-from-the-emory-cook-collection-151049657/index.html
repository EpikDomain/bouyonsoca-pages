A 2005 compilation of earlier material

First Flight gathers 18 selections from the Emory Cook collection. The Smithsonian booklet identifies the compilation as a 2005 edition, while its individual song headers carry years from 1957 to 1959. These are different date fields: a year printed beside a song is not automatically its exact session date, and the compilation’s release date should not replace it.

Earlier album references need their own dates

The booklet associates several song entries with Sparrow in Hi-Fi, while separately listing that earlier album edition as 1963. Preserve both observations rather than forcing the song-header years to match the album reference. The notes also point to Calypso Kings and Pink Gin and King Sparrow’s Calypso Carnival. Those references provide release history, without establishing that a current streaming master is identical to an earlier carrier.

Recording and compilation credits are different jobs

The booklet credits Emory Cook for recording, Gordon Rohlehr for compilation and annotation, and Pete Reiniger for sound supervision and mastering. Keep those roles attached to their documented scope. The writer note names Slinger Francisco, with Fitzroy Alexander additionally credited for “Sparrow vs. Melody Picong.”
