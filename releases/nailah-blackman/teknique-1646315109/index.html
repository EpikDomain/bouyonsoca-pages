Teknique

Nailah Blackman · 2022-09-23 (day precision; this storefront edition)

Navigate Teknique’s complete 19-track edition, its credited collaborators and the specifically labelled Come Home interlude, with release context from the 2022 promotional report.

A full-length project with a wide collaboration list

The Trinidad Guardian’s October 2022 report documented Nailah Blackman promoting Teknique at Slam 100.5 FM. It described the release as her first full-length album, with 19 tracks and collaborations spanning soca, dancehall and Afrobeats. Those genre descriptions belong to the report’s account of the project; they are not substituted for each recording’s own identity or credits.

Navigate the edition and its versions

The captured sequence credits Voice on Panorama, Walshy Fire and Puri on Say Less, and Father Philis and Salty on the title track. Kes joins Timeless near the end. Track 18 is specifically Come Home (Interlude), credited here to Blackman alone; that entry must not be silently replaced with the later duet or an acoustic version. The complete sequence runs from Best Friends to No One, with separate listening links for each entry.
