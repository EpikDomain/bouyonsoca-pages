Fini les foutaises

K-Reen · 2022-02-11 (day precision; this storefront edition)

Explore Fini les foutaises: its complete 21-track sequence, displayed performer credits and edition-specific listening links.

A guide to the featured tracks

The 21-track edition places the title song at track 6. Its feature labels include Still Fresh on “Triste mélodie,” Golen.B on “Fais ta life,” Axel Tony on “Sur tes lèvres,” and JmaX on “Cho patate.” DJ Eladji is named on “My Sexy Gwada” and, alongside Mokobé, on “Moonwalk.”

Following the full album sequence

“L’amour est mort” opens the listing and “Sensasyon” closes it. The track table keeps accented titles and each feature label visible, which helps distinguish a search for the album from a search for one collaboration. The February 11, 2022 date belongs to this catalog edition, not to a documented first release of every song.
