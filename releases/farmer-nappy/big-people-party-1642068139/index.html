Big People Party

Farmer Nappy · 2014-02-25 (day precision; this storefront edition)

Follow the twelve-track Big People Party edition, its label announcement and the distinction between Stranger and its road mix.

The label’s 2014 album announcement

FOX FUSE’s February 2014 announcement presented Big People Party as an album prepared across two years. Farmer Nappy explained that its name came from his favourite song on the record, while its intended audience crossed age groups. The announcement connected the project with De Red Boyz in Barbados, Precision Productions in Trinidad and Studio 758 in Saint Lucia. It listed the twelve songs and announced physical and digital availability for February 25. Those are the label’s contemporary statements about this album; they do not establish which producer handled each individual track.

Navigate the edition and its versions

The captured edition starts with Big People Party and places Stranger second. Stranger (Road Mix) appears separately at position ten; both entries remain in the sequence. Addiction names Imani in its title, and We Doh Sleep names Shurwayne Winchester. The announcement’s shorter tracklist omits those featured names, so the table preserves the fuller provider titles. Its announced February 25, 2014 release date agrees with the captured edition date, without making that date the first appearance of every song.
