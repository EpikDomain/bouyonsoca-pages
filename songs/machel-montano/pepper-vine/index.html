Behind the recording

Pepper Vine — vocal studios, production team and three named versions

The official “Pepper Vine” upload documents separate vocal sessions for Machel Montano, Drupatee and Lady Lava. Its studio credits help explain how the collaboration came together, while the selected single groups three distinctly labeled versions.

  • Where were the Pepper Vine vocals recorded, and which named versions are available?

From a selected beat to recorded vocals

DJ Private Ryan told Newsday that he and Montano selected the Bombay Tea Riddim from his beats. He developed the idea with Mela Caribe; Montano suggested Lady Lava, whose vocals were recorded in Toronto while she was travelling.

Sources: DJ Private Ryan — Pepper Vine creation interview, Newsday

Who recorded each vocalist?

Montano’s official upload names the engineers and locations below. These are audio-recording credits, not filming locations. They document different sessions without supplying exact session dates.

DJ Private Ryan and Jus Now receive production credit. The upload separately identifies additional production, background vocals and final audio processing, so those contributions need not be collapsed into a single producer label.

Choose the version by its full title

The US Apple Music single has an edition date of January 22, 2025 and three entries. “Hindi Version” and “Steam Version” are explicit provider labels. The unsuffixed entry is a separate listing; the shared package does not establish identical vocals or masters across all three.

The studio credits above belong to the official unsuffixed upload. A complete version-by-version credit comparison remains unavailable in these sources.
