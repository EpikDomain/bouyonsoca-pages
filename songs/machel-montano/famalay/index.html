Behind the recording

Famalay — Skinny Fabulous, Machel Montano and Bunji Garlin: studio credits

“Famalay” has separately published recording credits for Skinny Fabulous, Machel Montano and Bunji Garlin. The official artist upload names their engineers and studios, giving a more precise picture of the collaboration than a three-artist title alone.

Three vocalists, separately documented recording locations

The official SkinnyFabulousVEVO video description includes a distinct song-credit section. It places Skinny Fabulous’s recording work with Wildfire at Rebelmuzik Studio in New York and 4th Dimension in St. Vincent and the Grenadines.

Machel Montano is credited as recorded by Kasey Phillips at Monk Studio in Los Angeles. Bunji Garlin is credited as recorded by Jabari “Shawtboss” Winchester at BadBeagleStudios in Port of Spain. These are audio-recording credits; they do not establish that all three vocalists shared a session or that the video was filmed at those studios.
