Behind the recording

Fast Wine — Machel Montano: studios and credits

Machel Montano’s official audio upload identifies two recording locations for “Fast Wine”: Precision Productions in San Fernando and Advokit Productions in Port of Spain. Its credits distinguish the recording engineers from the production, mixing and mastering teams.

Audio credits

Kasey Phillips is credited with recording at Precision Productions, while Kit Israel recorded at Advokit Productions and supplied background vocals. P.A.G.E. and Precision Productions share the production and mixing credit; Dave Kutch handled mastering.

The writing credit names Jelani “Pops” Shaw, Kasey Phillips and Machel Montano. These roles come from the official audio description.
