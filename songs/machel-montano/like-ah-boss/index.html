Like Ah Boss — Machel Montano: the musicians and versions

Machel Montano’s official “Like Ah Boss” audio upload names the individual horn players as well as the producers, guitarist, recording team and backing vocalist.

Audio credits

The upload names Roy Cape All Star members Charles Williams on trumpet, Ian Hamilton on trombone and Garvin Marcelle on tenor sax. Kenny Phillips receives the additional guitar credit, and Akeem “Preedy” Chance is credited for additional backing vocals.

Deputy is the producer, with London Future and Machel Montano credited as co-producers. Full Blown Entertainment and Alex “Kubiyashi” Barnwell have separate additional-production credits.

Singles, albums and named versions

The selected Apple listings place the single on December 26, 2014 and the Monk Monté album on February 4, 2015. On that album it is track six. These are two release listings for the title; the dates alone do not establish a different performance or mix.
