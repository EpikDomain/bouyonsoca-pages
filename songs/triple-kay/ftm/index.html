Behind the recording

F.T.M — Triple Kay’s Fete Till Mornin’ credits and 2017 video

Triple Kay’s “F.T.M” means “Fete Till Mornin’.” The band’s 2017 statement documents both the audio production and the Hysteria Mas video collaboration, making it possible to credit each contribution separately.

  • Who sang and produced F.T.M, and who made its video?

From vocals to mastering

The band’s January 18, 2017 statement names Wayne “Mr. Benji N2O” Benjamin as writer and performer. Kendel “Killa” Laurent produced the track and mixed it at Music Box Studios, which is also the stated recording location.

A separately credited Carnival video

The same statement credits a video collaboration with Hysteria Mas, directed by Ericson Joseph of GremoPhoto. It announced a January 18 premiere; the band’s linked YouTube upload carries that date.

The band described the song as part of expanding its groovy catalog. Its official upload is labeled Soca 2017. That specific label is more useful here than assigning every Triple Kay release one genre automatically.
