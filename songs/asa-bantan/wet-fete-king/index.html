Wet Fete King — Asa Bantan: Chupidness Riddim and credits

Asa Bantan’s official “Wet Fete King” video names Chupidness Riddim and credits Lashley “Motto” Winter as producer. The same description separates the songwriter, executive producer and mixing/mastering credits.

Audio credits

Asa Bantan is the credited writer, Bashment Crew the executive producer, and DIZZLLE BUENO / NORTH ISLAND RECORDS the published mixing and mastering credit. The spelling below follows the artist’s description.

Naming the riddim connects this recording to a production credit in the source; it does not establish the full list of other songs built on that riddim.

Singles, albums and named versions

The selected Apple single edition is dated January 28, 2019. A later “Wet Fete King” listing appears on Di Bouyon Hot, Vol. 3, dated June 15, 2023. The compilation date describes that release package, not the beginning of the song’s history. Exact master equivalence between these listings has not been established.
