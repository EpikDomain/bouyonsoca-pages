Why does Play Mass appear with both 2020 and 2023 dates?

Apple's US catalog dates Play Mass (feat. Zone Rouge) - Single to 2020-01-28. It also lists Play Mass (feat. Zone Rouge) on Di Bouyon Hot, Vol. 3, dated 2023-06-15. Those dates describe separate catalog editions. Use the single link to hear that entry, or the compilation link to hear it within the album sequence; the later compilation date does not establish when the song was first recorded or first released worldwide.

Are these different recordings?

The two entries have separate Apple track identifiers. Both display a duration of about 3 minutes 52 seconds. Matching titles and similar running times are useful for finding the entries, but they do not prove identical audio, a remix, or a remaster. This guide preserves the two links so you can choose the edition explicitly.
