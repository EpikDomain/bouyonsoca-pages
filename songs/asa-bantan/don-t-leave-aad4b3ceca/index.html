Compare the single and compilation entries for Don't Leave, find the platform's contributor credits, and choose the exact release you want to hear.

Why does Don't Leave appear with both 2019 and 2023 dates?

Apple's US catalog dates Don't Leave - Single to 2019-01-23. It also lists Don't Leave on Di Bouyon Hot, Vol. 3, dated 2023-06-15. Those dates describe separate catalog editions. Use the single link to hear that entry, or the compilation link to hear it within the album sequence; the later compilation date does not establish when the song was first recorded or first released worldwide.

Are these different recordings?

The two entries have separate Apple track identifiers. Both display a duration of about 3 minutes 30 seconds. Matching titles and similar running times are useful for finding the entries, but they do not prove identical audio, a remix, or a remaster. This guide preserves the two links so you can choose the edition explicitly.
