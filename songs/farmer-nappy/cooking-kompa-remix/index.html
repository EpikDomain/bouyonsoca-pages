Cooking (Kompa Remix) — Shelly’s collaboration, instrumental credits and live version

The “Cooking” Kompa remix brings Farmer Nappy together with Shelly and North. Shelly’s account explains the collaboration and his growing confidence singing in Creole; separate studio and live sources show which performance each credit belongs to.

  • How did Shelly join Cooking, and how does the Kompa studio remix differ from the documented live performance?

A connection that preceded the remix

Shelly told Soca News in January 2026 that he had wanted to collaborate with Farmer Nappy for some time. He recalled Signal hiring Farmer for the band’s Lumi-Nation Carnival J’Ouvert troupe in 2018.

Discussing “Cooking” and his work with Krys on “Lè i Bon,” he described becoming more confident singing in Creole while continuing to learn the language. This is his account of an artistic development, rather than proof that the two songs share a recording session.

Sources: Shelly Alfred — Cooking collaboration and singing in Creole

Read the instrumental roles on the selected remix

Apple’s selected Kompa Remix page credits North as remixer, producer, mixing engineer and mastering engineer, and The Riddim Sektion as producer. It also supplies the individual instrumental and background-vocal roles below.

Shelly appears in the track’s featured-artist title but is absent from that page’s performer-role list. The title, his interview and the official live upload document his participation; the provider list should not be presented as a complete personnel roster.

Studio single and cruise performance

The studio single carries November 6, 2025 in Apple’s catalog. Farmer Nappy’s official live upload, published November 18, describes Farmer and Shelly performing the Kompa remix aboard the 2025 Uber Soca Cruise, backed by A Team Band.

The A Team credit belongs to that live performance. It should not replace the individual studio credits, and the video upload date is not an exact concert date.
