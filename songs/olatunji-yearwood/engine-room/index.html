Engine Room — Olatunji: musicians, production and brass refix

Olatunji’s official “Engine Room” video publishes a dedicated song-credit section. It names De Red Boyz as producer and identifies individual musicians across drums, keyboards, bass, pan, guitars, trumpet and trombone.

Audio credits

Michael Hulsmeier plays drums and Scott Galt keyboards. Nicholas Brancker is credited on bass and Terry “Mexican” Arthur on pan. Miguel Charles and Barry “Barman” Hill share the guitar credit, with Kevyn Lynch on trumpet and Jomo Slusher on trombone.

The writing credit names Jason “Shaft” Bishop, Scott Galt, Michael Hulsmeier and Olatunji Yearwood. Andrew Denny handled mixing and mastering. These are the audio credits; the video-production crew is listed separately in the source.

Singles, albums and named versions

The selected Apple listings distinguish Engine Room, dated December 26, 2022, from Engine Room (Allstars Brass Refix), dated February 10, 2023. The refix title signals a separately named version. The original video’s musician list should not automatically be assigned to the refix.
