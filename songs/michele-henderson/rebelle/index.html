Rebelle — Michele Henderson, Guy Suivant and the Martinique video

“Rebelle” connects Michele Henderson with songwriter Guy Suivant and a video filmed in Martinique. The artist announcement gives writing detail that is absent from the selected streaming credit list, making source attribution especially useful here.

  • Who wrote Rebelle’s English sections, and how do the video and streaming edition relate?

What the announcement says about the writing

The December 6, 2022 announcement reproduced by The Bajan Reporter names Guy Suivant as songwriter and credits Henderson with the English parts. Henderson’s accompanying comments frame the message around acknowledging faults and trying to improve oneself.

Apple’s selected song page lists Guy Suivant under Songwriter but does not show Henderson there. That difference is preserved: the announcement supplies an additional attributed contribution, while the provider page is presented as an observed credit list, not a complete account of authorship.

Sources: Michele Henderson — Rebelle release announcement with artist comments · ‎Rebelle - Song with Lyrics by Michele Henderson - Apple Music

The video has its own production credit

The announcement identifies Yannick Lina of Yanishi Recordz as director and places the visuals at locations around Martinique. Henderson’s official YouTube upload also credits Yanishi Recordz for the video.

The artist upload is titled “Rebèlle”; Apple uses “Rebelle.” These are source spellings for the selected material, rather than evidence of a separately recorded remix.

  • Michele Henderson — official Rebèlle video

Sources: Michele Henderson — Rebelle release announcement with artist comments · Michele Henderson — official Rebèlle video metadata · ‎Rebelle - Song with Lyrics by Michele Henderson - Apple Music

Three dates, three different records

The official video was uploaded before the announcement, which still described streaming availability as forthcoming. The selected Apple package, Zones de Turbulence - EP, carries December 13, 2022 and lists “Rebelle” first among six tracks.

The announcement and artist video description call the project Zone de Turbulence, in the singular. The table preserves the actual provider edition title in the plural; neither title variation establishes another audio version.
