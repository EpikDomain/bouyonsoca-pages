Tender — Patrice Roberts with A Team Band: acoustic credits

Patrice Roberts’ channel presents a distinct acoustic performance of “Tender” with Trinidad and Tobago’s A Team Band. It links back to a separate official music video, giving listeners a direct route between the two presentations.

A Team Band acoustic performance credits

The acoustic upload names Kareem Romain on percussion, Joshua Caines on keyboard and Miguel Charles on bass. Brennon Vergil receives the general “Audio” credit; the description does not break that role into recording, mixing or mastering.

These credits belong to this acoustic performance. They do not establish the personnel on the studio release or every other performance of “Tender.”
