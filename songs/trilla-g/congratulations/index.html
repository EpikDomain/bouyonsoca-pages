Behind the recording

Congratulations — Trilla-G’s writing spark, Th3rd and the live guitar credit

Trilla-G describes “Congratulations” as a song that grew from gratitude, a live conversation with fans and a friend’s writing response. The official upload then identifies the people who turned those ideas into the finished audio and video.

  • How did Congratulations begin, and who handled its vocals, guitar and production?

A live conversation became a chorus idea

In an Observer report published February 26, 2026, Trilla-G recalled encouraging listeners during a social-media live session to appreciate reaching 2026. A friend responded by writing material that became part of the chorus.

He said further lines came while he was in the shower. His account emphasizes gratitude and faith; it does not name that friend in the quoted writing passage, so the additional credits below should not be used to guess who supplied that initial idea.

Sources: Trilla-G — Observer AM account of Congratulations

The audio team has distinct roles

Trilla-G’s official video names Th3rd as both fellow performer and vocal engineer. Dernel Green receives production, arrangement and engineering credits, while Giovannie Green is credited with live guitars. These roles explain more than a two-name artist heading alone.

The film credits and upload date

The artist upload is dated February 7, 2026. It credits creative direction to Tridel Edwin, Anthony Nibbs and Gael Jno Baptiste; filming to 365MMP and A. Nibbs; and editing to A. Nibbs. Those are video roles, separate from the studio work.

The upload date and later interview date establish two public records. They do not identify the exact days on which writing or recording took place.
