Take Me As I Am — Trilla-G: production and remix guide

Trilla-G’s official video description credits Andel “Fr3EzE” Horsford with production and Dernel “Dernz” Green with additional production, recording and engineering on “Take Me As I Am.”

Audio credits

The writing credit uses Gael Jno Baptiste, while the performance credit uses Trilla-G. The description separately names the actress and video team; those are not audio-production roles.

The video description also identifies its introductory song as “Someone Else” by Litleboy, Quan and Trilla-G. That introductory excerpt is separate from the title recording.

Singles, albums and named versions

Apple lists a Take Me As I Am single dated September 18, 2024. The video title’s “Bouyon 2025” wording therefore should not replace the digital-edition date.

Later Apple listings explicitly mark a V’ghn remix: a single dated October 11, 2025 and an appearance on Take Me As I Am: The Album dated October 22, 2025. The original video’s production credits are not automatically credits for that remix.
