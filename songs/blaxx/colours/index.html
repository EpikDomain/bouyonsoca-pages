Colours — the collaboration and studio credits

Blaxx connected “Colours” with Leroy Clarke and two Grenadian collaborators.

  • What did Blaxx say inspired Colours, and who handled the different studio roles?

The conversation at Legacy House

Blaxx recalled discussing social breakdown and Carnival’s roots with Leroy Clarke during a Legacy House photoshoot. This prompted his power-soca ambitions. He described working with Grenadian writers Hitz and Kimoney as an expression of Caribbean unity.

Sources: Newsday — Blaxx interview

The specifically identified single

Apple identifies the selected song as “Colours” by Blaxx and Andrew ‘Hitz’ Phillip. The single footer carries January 19, 2016 and a Therapist Music rights notice; the interview was published January 24.

This pairing anchors a particular song and collaboration. It does not resolve the unrelated repertoire found on other pages sharing the name Blaxx, or supply individual instrumentalists absent from these sources.
