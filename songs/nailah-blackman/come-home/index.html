Behind the recording

Come Home — the Carnival story, production team and named mixes

Andre Jeffers explains “Come Home” through the feeling of missing Carnival. The Nailah Blackman and Skinny Fabulous collaboration has a documented production team and several explicitly titled versions, each worth identifying before pressing play.

  • What inspired Come Home, and which of its road, world and acoustic versions am I selecting?

Carnival’s absence became the story

In Newsday’s January 24, 2023 interview, Jeffers described personifying the absence of Carnival as a story of longing. Producer Kyle Phillips said the work developed across pandemic studio sessions alongside “The Return” and “The Ambush.”

Jeffers distinguished the songs’ emotional purposes: “Come Home” made the plea for Carnival’s return. This explains the writer’s intended story without treating its narrator as a literal account of either performer’s relationship.

Sources: Kyle Phillips and Andre Jeffers — Come Home production account

Audio work and video work

The official artist upload names Kyle Phillips, Kitwana Israel, Mevon Soodeen and Anson Pro as producers. Newsday additionally identifies Gregory Ayuen and Karyce Phillips on background vocals, Dre Major for mixing and Parry Jack for mastering.

The February 8 official video credits direction to Keyon Mitchell and Anson Pro. Direction is a separate contribution from the audio production; the video timestamp does not date the studio sessions.
