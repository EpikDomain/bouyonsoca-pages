Behind the recording

Truck On D Road — Bunji Garlin: solo listings and A$AP Ferg remix

The A$AP Ferg feature is the clearest way to identify the remix of “Truck On D Road.” VP Records explicitly describes it as a remix, names Doc and Jes as its producers, and places it fifth on Differentology.

The documented collaboration

VP’s announcement reports that Garlin met A$AP Ferg in Trinidad during Carnival 2014. It credits Funkmaster Flex with premiering the remix through Hot 97 and InFlexWeTrust. The announcement gives August 12, 2014 as the album’s planned release date.

The Doc and Jes production credit in that announcement belongs to the remix. It should not be carried over automatically to the earlier EP tracks.

Sources: VP Records — Truck On D Road remix announcement

Singles, albums and named versions

Apple Music lists tracks titled “Truck On D Road” on Carnival Tabanca and Ready For the Road. Its Differentology entry adds the A$AP Ferg feature, corresponding to the version named in VP’s album announcement. The two earlier EP listings have no featured-artist suffix; their exact master relationship remains unconfirmed.
