Behind the recording

Identity — vocal and pan arrangements

Mark Loquan distinguishes Denyse Plummer’s vocal recording from the steelband arrangements of “Identity.”

  • Who created Identity, and which arrangement belongs to the vocal recording?

A melody presented to Plummer’s team

After returning from Norway, Loquan recalls presenting the melody to Plummer’s team with Christophe Grant present. It began three successive years working with Grant, exploring pan and West Indian identity.

Sources: Mark Loquan — Identity

Keep the three settings separate

Loquan identifies three different arrangements. Their credits belong to the respective settings, rather than one combined recording team.
