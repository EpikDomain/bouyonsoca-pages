Behind the recording

Splinters — the 2 A.M. Project, audio credits and cruise video

“Splinters” separates studio and video crews, with different dates for filming, Carnival and album appearances.

  • Who made Splinters, and what do its different dates describe?

Start with the song credits

Dori Pro’s official-video description places “Splinters” on the 2 A.M. Project Riddim. It names Problem Child as writer and Ramon “Fryktion” McDowall as producer, with recording at Sheppard Pro Studios and mixing and mastering by Precision Productions.

The selected Apple entries use a more detailed contributor roster: Ramon McDowall as composer, Shertz James and Stephenson Marshall under lyrics, and Fryktion Muzik as producer. These are the credit lists supplied by each source; the shorter video description should not be expanded into an exclusive songwriting claim.

The cruise footage has its own crew

The upload identifies the 2017 Uber Soca Cruise as the filming location and calls the song a 2018 Trinidad Carnival release. Cowin Thorpe directed and edited; Thorpe and Melvern Isaac handled camera and photography. These are video credits.

Sources: Dori Pro — Splinters

Riddim release and later album appearance

Apple dates the 2:00AM Project entry November 17, 2017, while the iShal entry carries February 8, 2019. Choose the carrier you want; a later album appearance does not establish that the composition or video originated in 2019.
