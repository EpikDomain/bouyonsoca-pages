Behind the recording

God Made Us All — Lord Invader and Pete Seeger: the 1946 performance

“God Made Us All” brings Lord Invader and Pete Seeger together in the Smithsonian’s Calypso in New York selection. The session header identifies the Union Hootenanny in New York on May 9, 1946.

Vocal, spoken introduction and banjo

Lord Invader receives the vocal credit. Pete Seeger is credited for speech and banjo, while the clarinet player is unidentified. These distinct roles explain why the catalog names both Seeger and Invader for the track.
