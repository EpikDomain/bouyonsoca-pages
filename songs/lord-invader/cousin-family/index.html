Behind the recording

Cousin Family — Lord Invader: the Felix ensemble recording

Lord Invader’s “Cousin Family” appears with Felix and his Internationals on Calypso in New York. The booklet distinguishes it from a different Destroyer song bearing the same title.

An approximate 1947 session

The notes place this performance in New York around July or August 1947. Gregory Felix is named on clarinet; the cuatro, trumpet, fiddle, bass and chorus performers are not individually identified.
