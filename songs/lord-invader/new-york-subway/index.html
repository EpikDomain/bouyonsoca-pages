Behind the recording

New York Subway — Lord Invader: session musicians and earlier issues

“New York Subway” is documented as a Lord Invader performance with Felix and his Internationals, recorded in New York around January 1946. The session notes name both its clarinet and cuatro players.

Who plays on this recording?

Gregory Felix plays clarinet and Patrick McDonald-Macbeth plays cuatro. The trumpet, fiddle and bass players remain unnamed in this source. Its approximate month should not be converted into an exact recording day.
