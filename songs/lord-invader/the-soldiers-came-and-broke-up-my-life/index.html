Behind the recording

The Soldiers Came and Broke Up My Life — Lord Invader: version 2

The Calypso in New York booklet labels this performance “The Soldiers Came and Broke Up My Life (#2).” The current label tracklist shortens the title to “The Soldiers Came and Broke My Life.”

A probable cuatro credit

The session header places the performance in New York around 1956. It tentatively identifies Cecil Anderson, the Duke of Iron, on cuatro. That attribution remains probable; the fiddle and bass players are unnamed.
