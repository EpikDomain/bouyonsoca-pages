My Intention Is War — Lord Invader and Mighty Dictator: recording credits

“My Intention Is War” is credited to Lord Invader and Mighty Dictator in the Smithsonian tracklist. Its session notes place both singers in a New York recording from around 1958.

Two singers and three named instrumental credits

Cecil Anderson, the Duke of Iron, is credited on piano. The guitarist and bassist appear only as Lawrence and Ford respectively. Those names are reproduced at the level the source establishes; full identities are unresolved.
