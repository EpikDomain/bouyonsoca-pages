Behind the recording

You’re Wasting Your Time — Lord Invader: the 1961 alternative take

The Smithsonian booklet identifies this selection as “You’re Wasting Your Time (#2) (alternative take).” It dates the New York recording to June 6, 1961.

Identify the take before comparing versions

Lord Invader is the named vocalist. Piano, guitar, saxophone, bass, maracas and drums are listed without individual player names. The current label tracklist uses the shorter title “You’re Wasting Your Time.”
