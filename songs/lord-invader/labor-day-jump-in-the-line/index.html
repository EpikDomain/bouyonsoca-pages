Behind the recording

Labor Day (Jump in the Line) — Lord Invader: the circa 1948 recording

“Labor Day” appears as “Labor Day (Jump in the Line)” in the Calypso in New York booklet. The documented New York recording dates to approximately April 1948.

Piano attribution and surviving references

The notes tentatively credit Daphne Weeks on piano alongside Lord Invader’s vocal. The word “probably” matters: this source does not establish a certain pianist identification.

Find the documented performance

The booklet gives matrix CB217, a Smithsonian acetate reference and two Folkways catalog references. It explicitly leaves the original Cub issue number unknown. The later 2000 compilation therefore offers a traceable performance without resolving every earlier release detail.
