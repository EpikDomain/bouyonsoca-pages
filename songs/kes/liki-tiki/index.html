Behind the recording

Liki Tiki — Kes: the single, reggae remix and album appearance

“Liki Tiki” appears in several release settings. J Perry’s official song page dates the single to March 18, 2022, while Ineffable Records separately dates the named reggae remix to April 28, 2023.

The documented collaboration

J Perry’s site credits the single to Kes, J Perry and Michael Brun. Dwala’s own selected-works page confirms his production contribution to “Liki Tiki.”

The Ineffable remix listing names Maoli, J Boog, J Perry and Michael Brun alongside Kes. That additional lineup belongs to the named remix and should not be read back into the original single credit.

Sources: J Perry — Liki Tiki single · Dwala — selected production credits · Ineffable Records — Liki Tiki reggae remix

Singles, albums and named versions

Apple’s selected reggae-remix edition contains two tracks: the named remix followed by “Liki Tiki.” A later listing places “Liki Tiki” on Man With No Door in 2024. The album appearance is useful for finding the song, but its later date is not evidence of a newly recorded performance.
