Behind the recording

Nasty Up — Problem Child’s staging account and the Wetty Beatz Remix

Problem Child described a deliberately minimal “Nasty Up” semifinal presentation: himself, the song and four dancers. That account concerns a live performance; the catalog separately identifies the Darkseid Riddim entry and a Wetty Beatz Remix.

  • What did Problem Child say about staging Nasty Up, and which studio version should I select?

A minimal semifinal presentation

In an exclusive February 4, 2020 ANN interview, Problem Child, identified as Shertz James, said he used the semifinal presentation to test the song’s impact with four dancers and little additional staging.

He hoped to develop the visual presentation for the final, but described funding as a constraint. Those were plans at the time of the interview, not evidence that a particular final-stage design was completed. The article also does not identify which studio mix supplied the semifinal backing track.

Sources: Problem Child — Nasty Up semifinal staging interview, ANN

Audio roles on the selected song pages

Both selected Apple song pages identify Phillip Bastien as composer and Shertz James under Lyrics. Wetty-Beatz Productionz receives producer credit on both; the named remix page additionally lists Wetty-Beatz Productionz as remixer.

These source-specific roles provide an identity link to the Shertz James interviewed by ANN. They do not authorize merging unrelated performers who also use the name Problem Child.
