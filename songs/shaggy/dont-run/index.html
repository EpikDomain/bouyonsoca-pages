Behind the recording

Don’t Run — Shaggy’s long-held song with Skinny Fabulous and its EP placement

Shaggy said “Don’t Run” had been waiting for the right moment for about six years. Its collaboration with Skinny Fabulous became part of In the Mood, but that recollection is different evidence from the dated single and EP listings.

  • Was Don’t Run created for the 2023 EP, or did Shaggy already have the song?

An older idea found its moment

In his February 19, 2023 Newsday interview, Shaggy said he had “Don’t Run” with Skinny Fabulous for about six years without releasing it. He described revisiting unreleased material for a Carnival-influenced project that he wanted to work beyond a single season.

That recollection does not establish a precise 2017 recording date, nor whether the released vocals and production were identical to an earlier version. It explains why a song discussed as a new release could have a much longer development history.

Sources: Shaggy — why Don’t Run waited for the right project

The selected single’s audio credits

Apple’s single page credits Shaggy as performer and Skinny Fabulous for vocals. Its composition list names Ashante Reid, Gamal Doyle, Orville Burrell, Ricardo Lloyd Johnson Jr and Shane Hoosong; Burrell and Hoosong also receive production credit.

These are the roles displayed for this single entry. The source does not divide the composers’ contributions or identify the dates of their sessions.

The single preceded the EP

The Apple single displays February 17, 2023. VP Records identifies “Don’t Run” as track five on the seven-track In the Mood, released May 12, 2023 jointly with Ranch Entertainment.

The February interview had discussed an expected March project release. The later label announcement provides the May release date; an early plan should not be substituted for that later record.
