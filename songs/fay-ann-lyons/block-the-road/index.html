Behind the recording

Block the Road — Fay-Ann Lyons and Stonebwoy: video setting and credits

VP Records describes “Block the Road” as a Fay-Ann Lyons track featuring Stonebwoy and gives its video an important geographical distinction: it was filmed in Jamaica while portraying Ghana.

Jamaica was the filming location; Ghana was the portrayed setting

VP Records identifies Terminal 4 Media as director of the “Block the Road” video and says it was filmed in Jamaica while portraying Ghana. The label also names Afro Pop as the outlet that premiered the video.

That distinction answers two different viewing questions: where the production took place and where its story is set. Ghana should not be substituted for the filming location, and neither location establishes where the audio was recorded.

The album entry and its mastering credit

Apple Music identifies the track as “Block the Road (feat. Stonebwoy)” on Fay-Ann Lyons’s Break the World. Its contributor panel names Paul Shields as mastering engineer. The same panel contains graphic-design, cover-art and photography entries, which are not instrumental or audio-production roles.

This source set does not establish a recording studio or audio producer. Terminal 4 Media’s documented role is video director, not a substitute for either missing recording credit.
