Behind the recording

Liar Liar — Destra’s meeting with Tanya Stephens and the Queen recording

Destra’s account of “Liar Liar” begins with asking Tanya Stephens for an opinion on a song. Their collaboration gives the selected Queen recording a documented creation story beyond its featured-artist label.

  • How did Destra and Tanya Stephens decide to collaborate on Liar Liar?

An opinion became a collaboration

Speaking to LargeUp for its May 23, 2016 premiere, Destra recalled meeting Tanya Stephens in St Lucia about two years earlier. She was exploring reggae and played Stephens a song she had been developing, asking what she thought.

Destra said Stephens liked the material and described their easy connection. The premiere identifies Jason “J-Vibe” Farmer as producer. That production attribution comes from the contemporary premiere, not from the shorter streaming credit list.

Sources: Destra — Liar Liar premiere interview with Tanya Stephens

What the selected streaming entry adds

Apple lists the performer as Destra Garcia and titles the track “Liar Liar (feat. Tanya Stephens).” Its writing section shows Destra Garcia and the spelling Tanya Stephenson. The provider spelling is retained here as an observation; it is not used to infer a separate person or a legal-name correction.

The song page does not list a producer in its displayed credit sections. That omission does not contradict the premiere’s explicit J-Vibe credit.

Sources: ‎Liar Liar (feat. Tanya Stephens) - Song with Lyrics by Destra Garcia - Apple Music · Destra — Liar Liar premiere interview with Tanya Stephens

Locate the ten-track Queen edition

The selected Queen package contains ten tracks and places “Liar Liar” last, after “Real Love” featuring Peetah Morgan. Apple displays March 14, 2016 for the edition, while the LargeUp premiere was published in May.

These dates identify a provider package and a later editorial premiere. The word premiere does not, by itself, establish the first worldwide availability of the recording.
