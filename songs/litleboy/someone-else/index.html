Someone Else — Litleboy, Quan and Trilla-G: collaboration and credits

“Someone Else” brings together Litleboy, Quan and Trilla-G. Its published credits give Litle Boy several production roles, and Trilla-G separately identifies the song as the intro music in his “Take Me As I Am” video.

The collaboration and Litle Boy’s published roles

DJTaffy Tha Riddim Beast’s promotional upload names Litle Boy, Quan and Trilla G as the writers and singers. It singles out Litle Boy for the chorus, riddim production, mixing, mastering and executive production, and names Turnt Up Tv Studios as the recording location.

These credits come from a promoter’s upload, not an assumed artist-owned channel. A separate artist source supports the collaboration itself: Trilla-G’s official “Take Me As I Am” description names “Someone Else” by Litleboy, Quan and Trilla-G as its intro song.

How the streaming entry identifies the song

Apple Music lists the single as “Someone Else (feat. Quan & Trilla-G)” under litleboy lsbeats767 and dates its song entry July 5, 2024. The contributor fields name Foster Marcel Xavier, Gael Jno Baptiste and Quan Denis as songwriters, with litleboy lsbeats767 as producer.

The promotional names and provider names are displayed with their source labels. This guide does not assign each legal name to a stage name or claim that the promotional upload and streaming entry have been matched to the same audio master.

Recognizing its appearance before Take Me As I Am

If you encountered “Someone Else” at the beginning of Trilla-G’s “Take Me As I Am” video, the artist’s own intro-song credit identifies the connection. It does not make the two titles alternate names for one recording. The main song’s Fr3EzE and Dernz production credits should not be transferred to “Someone Else.”
