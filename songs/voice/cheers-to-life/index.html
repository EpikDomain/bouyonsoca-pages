Behind the recording

Cheers to Life — Voice’s recording and the separate SocaKidz version

“Cheers to Life” appears under Voice and Precision Productions and, separately, under SocaKidz. Producer testimony explains the later children’s recording, so the shared song title need not obscure the different performers.

  • Is the SocaKidz Cheers to Life the same vocal recording as Voice’s version?

Voice’s account before the 2016 final

In Newsday’s January 31, 2016 Q&A, Voice explained that he wanted his music to carry a message while challenging himself melodically. He connected that aim to “Cheers to Life.”

He also recalled hearing the Soca Monarch semifinal audience respond to his name before the instrumental began, a moment that eased his nerves. This is an artist’s account of that performance; the interview was written before the final and does not document its outcome.

Sources: Voice — Cheers to Life Q&A, Newsday

New vocals, and a separate video cast

Kasey Phillips told Newsday that SocaKidz reused existing Precision and Advokit music beds but recorded new vocals in July–August 2019. The album’s singers were Akeela Dillon, Daniel Garavito and Antonio Wade, selected by Stacy Nurse of Rosemand’s Academy of Performing Art.

Those names are given at album level, without a per-song solo allocation. The article lists “Cheers to Life” as originally by Voice and names Steven Taylor as director of the SocaKidz video. Phillips explicitly says the recording singers and the children appearing in the video were different performers.

Sources: Kasey Phillips — SocaKidz re-recording and video interview, Newsday

Choose by the credited performing artist

Apple’s selected Voice entry credits Precision Productions as producer. The selected SocaKidz page lists both Precision Productions and BadJohn Republic in its production section. Both pages show the same five writing contributors, while their performing-artist headings differ.

The provider dates below belong to the selected listings. Neither one turns the SocaKidz vocals into a Voice performance or identifies the children seen in the video as the recording singers.
