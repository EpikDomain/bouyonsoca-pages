Appreciation — Andy Narell: Panorama context and two album listings

“Appreciation” was written for the 2000 Panorama, according to Andy Narell. The later catalog question is which album entry you want: Fire in the Engine Room and Tatoom list different groups of featured collaborators.

A composition written for the 2000 Panorama

Andy Narell tells When Steel Talks that he wrote “Appreciation” for the 2000 Panorama. He identifies it as the closing track of Tatoom. The competition occasion explains the composition’s background, but it does not identify the album track as a recording made at that competition.

The interview does not establish a competition result or name the Panorama ensemble for this piece. Those details remain outside this guide rather than being inferred from the year.

Sources: When Steel Talks — Narell on Appreciation and Tatoom

Two album listings with different named collaborators

Apple Music lists “Appreciation” on Fire in the Engine Room with Louis Mhlanga, Keith Jones, Paul van Wageningen and Jesus Diaz in the track title. Its Tatoom listing instead names Luis Conte and Mark Walker. Those different guest labels provide a practical way to choose between the two album entries.

The captured Tatoom song page credits Narell as performer and songwriter. Its title names the guests, but the song’s contributor panel does not give their instrumental roles. The different album titles and guest lists are preserved as separate listings; they do not establish the precise recording or master relationship between the two.
