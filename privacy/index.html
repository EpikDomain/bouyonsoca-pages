Simple by design

This version of BouyonSoca does not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts, email collection or user accounts. Search and genre filtering run in your browser. The search query and selected genre are reflected in the page URL.

Your preferences

If you change the motion setting, the site saves that preference in your browser's local storage. You can clear it through your browser's site-data settings. The site also respects your device's reduced-motion preference.

External services

Music and source links open third-party sites. Their privacy policies, cookies and account requirements apply when you visit them. The hosting provider may process technical access information to serve and protect the site. If you are viewing a private preview, its host may require sign-in.
