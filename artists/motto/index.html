Behind the music

The story so far.

Motto is Saint Lucian producer, songwriter and performer Lashley Wendell Winter, from Grace in Vieux Fort. In his Dazzle interview, he recalled moving to the United States in 2009 and beginning by sending beats to artists back in Saint Lucia. He identifies his production with Dennery Segment and describes choosing where to use Kwéyòl so listeners can follow the songs. His work includes the PIM PIM Riddim, associated with Machel Montano, Skinny Fabulous, Lyrikal and his own Party Lit. Man with Ride and One Woman are two selected listening points from his provider catalog. The selected Man with Ride edition names BlackBoy and Ezra in its title and credits Motto and Team Foxx as producers; its Roadmix remains separately identified. Those selected associations do not resolve every release currently attached to the same provider artist name.
