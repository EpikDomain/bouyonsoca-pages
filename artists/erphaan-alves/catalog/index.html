Erphaan Alves
Release catalog

Browse the captured releases, compare tracklists, and listen through the credited platform links.

Most of Erphaan Alves’s captured catalog arrives as single-track editions, many shared with other performers. Use numbered parts and acoustic labels to tell related titles apart, then check the riddim EPs and compilations for his individual rows.

Separate numbered parts and acoustic versions

Spirit, Spirit, Pt. 2 and Spirit Pt 3 are three separate single editions. The third lists Erphaan Alves, Wuss Ways and Runi Jay, with Kes Keyz, DJ Kingston and 1st Klase featured in its title. Anchor Down and Anchor Down (Acoustic) both display September 21, 2023. Keep each labelled part and version as its own row rather than as one song with alternate names.

Find his rows on riddims and compilations

We Muzik: Soca 2012 Trinidad and Tobago Carnival, Vol. 1 names Erphaan Alves on In Your Eyes, On D Spot and Terrible, the last also crediting Machel Montano. Overdue has its own single, while Overdue (Road Mix) sits on We Muzik: Soca 2018 Trinidad and Tobago Carnival, Vol. 9. Lifeline with Tano is both a single and the fourth row of High Street Riddim.

What this source selection covers

Shared releases retain all displayed performers. Edition dates are not necessarily first-release dates.
