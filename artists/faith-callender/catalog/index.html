Release catalog

Browse the captured releases, compare tracklists, and listen through the credited platform links.

Faith Callender’s captured listing is mostly single-track editions, plus the Chapter 33 album, the Memories EP and many shared riddims. Use repeated titles to move between album, single and riddim editions without assuming they are the same recording.

Chapter 33 and its related singles

Chapter 33 has 11 rows, opening with From Nothing and including Keep It, both of which also have single editions. Its fourth row, Bajan Code (feat. Dj Spider), shares its title with Bajan Code - Single, which pairs a main row with a Radio Edit, and with row two of Dj Spider’s Don Gorgon Riddim - EP. Matching titles do not establish identical recordings.

Same titles, separate editions

Baddie has two single editions, displaying March 20 and March 22, 2021, with different rights lines. Thunder Clap and Thunderclap are separate singles that both display September 30, 2022. Inside out Riddim lists Werk twice, at rows five and 12, each credited to Faith Callender & Jus D Music with the same duration and no distinguishing label.

Shared releases retain all displayed performers. Edition dates are not necessarily first-release dates.
