Behind the music

The story so far.

Fanny J is a singer from Cayenne, French Guiana, whose music combines contemporary zouk with an expressive R&B sensibility. Local singing competitions helped introduce her to the producer Warren, an important collaborator in the development of her recording career. Ancrée à ton port became a defining song from her debut album Vous les hommes, establishing a repertoire centred on love, vulnerability and the strength of her vocal delivery. Secrets de femme followed with songs including Okay, while Mes vérités continued her album catalogue. Her music connects Guyanese talent with audiences across the French Caribbean and mainland France. Start with Ancrée à ton port and Okay to hear how she balances intimate storytelling with a rhythm made for dancing.
