This Drupatee catalog follows the specifically reviewed Apple principal, including In Style 2000, Dekho Lawa, short vocal-and-instrumental releases and shared compilations. Other Drupatee provider profiles remain separate, so this inventory should not be read as the whole historical discography.

Browse the longer editions and paired tracks

In Style 2000 contains 11 tracks and Dekho Lawa eight in the captured editions. Chutney Parang has a vocal row and a separately labelled instrumental. Chamkay pairs Chamkay (Wine de Waist), credited to Drupatee, with an instrumental credited to Millbeatz Entertainment. Mohan contains three entries, including two differently worded instrumental titles; keep each displayed title intact.

Keep a featured appearance inside its shared sequence

Pepper Vine retains three version labels and the feature wording naming Drupatee and Jus Now. Indian Gyal (feat. Drupatee) appears within Machel Montano’s Machelements, Vol. 1, while Curry Que Riddim contains Bollywood credited to Drupatee and K Rich alongside songs credited to other performers. These complete shared tracklists do not make every row a Drupatee performance.
