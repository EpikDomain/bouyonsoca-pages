Black Stalin
Release catalog

Browse the captured releases, compare tracklists, and listen through the credited platform links.

Black Stalin’s captured catalog is small: three albums under his name plus two multi-artist releases. Read the albums for his own sequences, then use the performer column to find the few rows that name him on Machel Montano’s and Roy Cape’s releases.

Read the three album sequences

Roots, Rock, Soca has 11 rows, running from Caribbean Unity to Kaiso Gone Dread, and displays January 1, 1991. Message To Sundar has 10, opening with Where De Man. Just for You has nine and ends with Dance Mix rows for One Slave and Andre the Lion, titles that also appear unlabelled earlier in that sequence. These are provider edition dates, not original-release dates.

Find the rows that name him on shared releases

The Xtatik Parade is a 15-track Machel Montano album; Black Stalin appears only in Love Fire (feat. Black Stalin, Farmer Nappy & Fresh Life). Roy Cape: A Calypso and Soca Anthology has 18 rows. Black Stalin is the displayed performer on Kaiso Gone Dread (feat. Roy Cape) and Leroy, Roy (with Roy Cape), and is featured on Name the Game by Roy Cape All Stars. Other rows belong to other performers.
