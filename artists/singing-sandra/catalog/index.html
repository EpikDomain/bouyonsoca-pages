Singing Sandra
Release catalog

Browse the captured releases, compare tracklists, and listen through the credited platform links.

Singing Sandra’s captured catalog centers on the 12-track Clean Heart album, with three short singles and three multi-artist collections. On the collections, use the performer column to find the single row that names her in each sequence.

Clean Heart and the Central Records singles

Clean Heart runs 12 rows from its title track to For Whom the Bell Tolls, with Ancient Rythms at position seven, spelled as the provider lists it. Cleansing Fire and Journey of the Enabler each pair a vocal row with an instrumental credited to Central Records; the Journey of the Enabler vocal names Dale Ryan & Singing Sandra. One Day Without a Murder is a one-row single.

Find her row on each compilation

Sweet Sweet Soca lists Die with Dignity at row 10, Carnival in Trinidad lists Voices from De Ghetto at row five, and Goodwill Messengers of the Red White and Black lists Equal Justice at row 11. Every other row on these three collections names a different performer, so treat them as shared releases rather than Singing Sandra albums.
