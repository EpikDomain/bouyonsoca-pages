Compare the differently ordered Couleurs du monde editions before choosing a track. Then use the radio-edit, instrumental and live labels in shorter releases to navigate Tina Ly’s appearances without merging repeats or assigning an entire shared release to her.

Choose the intended Couleurs du monde edition

Couleurs du monde: Gold Collector has 16 rows and starts with Sé'y ou inmé. Couleurs du monde (Collector) has 15 and starts with Pa ni tan (bonus track inédit), credited to Tina Ly & Lynnsha. The unsuffixed Couleurs du monde also has 15, but opens with Intro. Gold Collector and the unsuffixed edition display January 1, 2007; Collector displays October 5, 2009. These are provider edition dates, not a resolved original-release chronology.

Keep repeat titles and guest appearances distinct

Fallais te quitter lists a Radio Edit, an unsuffixed vocal title and an Instrumental, retaining the Tina Ly & Vayb performer string. Douce folie contains two identically titled rows followed by Douce folie (Instrumentale); the source does not label a difference between the first two. Ji Kann’s five-track Live for you (live) EP names Tina Ly on Reste encore un peu, while other rows name Guy-Andre or Gregz. Keep the complete sequence without assigning every live performance to Tina Ly.
