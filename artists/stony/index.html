Behind the music

The story so far.

Stony is a singer from Guadeloupe whose repertoire includes Dança Kizomba, T’en aller with Oswald and Call Me. In Mouv’s August 2024 studio appearance, she discussed the difficult initial reception of Dança Kizomba before its international reach. The broadcaster also traced collaborations with Mac Tyer and Marvin and presented her live performance of Call Me. A separate July 2024 La1ère report described Call Me as zouk R&B and traced her repertoire from Loin de Moi in 2010 through Mes Emotions in 2011 to Authentique in 2015. The live radio performance and commercial listening editions provide different ways to encounter her work.
