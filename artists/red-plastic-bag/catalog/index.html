Red Plastic Bag’s captured listing centers on three albums credited jointly with Mac Fingall, plus the single I Can't Imagine and rows on compilations and another artist’s album. Keep Mac Fingall’s credit with every album row.

Read the three joint albums

One More has 13 rows, Bajan Invasion 12 and Happiness nine, each row credited to Red Plastic Bag & Mac Fingall. One More pairs Rake Shake at row two with Rake Shake (Congo Version) at row 13. The provider dates read 1996, 1996 and 1993, while each rights line reads ℗ 2020 WIRL; neither is treated as an original-release date.

Find his rows on compilations and other artists’ releases

Ragga Ragga opens Happiness and also sits at row four of Soca for de World. Soca Gold 2009 lists Wrong Gal at row 15, and Kirk Brown’s Ah Ready opens with Don't Have to Wine (feat. Red Plastic Bag). The other rows on these releases belong to other performers, and matching titles do not establish identical recordings.
