Keep disc numbers and displayed performers visible

Bipolaire places Black at disc one, track one and Cacahuète (feat. Nicy) at disc two, track one. Disc one has 14 rows and disc two has 11; the numbering restart is not a duplicate to remove. Sky Is the Limit lists 22 tracks, including a ninth row, Un dos tres, whose performer is printed as Keron. Preserve that spelling without assuming an alias. SHATTA HARD starts with Shootè la and Tchou Tchou, both credited to Keros-N & Youth CrazySquad.

Distinguish a paired single from a named version

Open The Door + Caïpi - Single contains two different titles, each credited to Keros-N & AMK. Ainsi font appears at position seven on SHATTA HARD with Keros-N & Régi, while the separate Ainsi font - Hard version single retains that shared performer wording. Compare the explicit version labels without treating a shared title and credit as proof of identical recordings.
