Raymond Ramnarine’s inventory connects individually issued songs with Dil-E-Nadan collaborations, instrumental counterparts and compilation appearances. Read the performer string as carefully as the title: the source varies between solo, joint and featured credits, and those differences are retained here.

Compare the named edition rather than merging a title

Nack ah Ting is credited to Dil-E-Nadan and Raymond Ramnarine, while Nack ah Ting (Unity Remix) is credited to Motto and Raymond Ramnarine. Show Me has a vocal and instrumental row carrying the shared Dil-E-Nadan credit. Mere Dil Ne Tadap Ke pairs Anil Bheem and Raymond Ramnarine across its two rows. The display names describe these editions, not a legal identity or master-recording merge.

Use the shared song and its surrounding sequence

Rags credits Raymond Ramnarine, DJ Private Ryan and Jus Now. Tassa Man and Tassa Man (Extended Intro) are separately titled releases crediting Jadel, Raymond Ramnarine and Millbeatz. Get Soca 2021 supplies a complete compilation sequence with other performers retained. These appearances expand browsing without claiming the full Dil-E-Nadan catalog or assigning every compilation song to Raymond.

What this inventory covers

This inventory contains 146 track appearances across 82 captured editions of the reviewed US Apple catalog, including single-track releases and the complete sequences of shared releases. A song can appear more than once. Dates identify the provider edition, not necessarily its first release. The lookup requested up to 200 collections; source shelves and their pagination limits remain visible in the coverage record. Physical releases, other territories, alternate profiles and unlinked collaborations may be missing.
