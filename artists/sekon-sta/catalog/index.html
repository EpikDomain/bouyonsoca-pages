Sekon Sta’s inventory connects individual songs with vocal-and-instrumental releases, named intros and shared riddim sequences. It is designed for finding a particular displayed version and its surrounding tracks, including appearances whose release belongs to several performers.

Use the intro and instrumental labels

Wayward (Remix) - EP contains Wayward, Ryan Sayeed’s Wicked Intro, Ryan Sayeed’s Clap Intro and a Remix row, all credited to Ricardo Drue and Sekon Sta. Champagne & Soca includes a Mega Mick Intro and an instrumental credited to Mega Mick. My World has two rows credited to Sekon Sta; Magic pairs Nadia Batson and Sekon Sta with a separate Mega Mick instrumental.

Locate a song inside a shared riddim

The Best occupies track three of the seven-row Kan Kan Riddim. Behavior Stink opens Moko Jumbie Riddim, while Masterpiece closes the four-row Tamboo Town Riddim. Reading those sequences preserves the neighbouring performers and makes clear which song is Sekon Sta’s appearance. Compilation rows contribute to this inventory’s appearance count, not to a count of his unique recordings.

What this inventory covers

This inventory contains 241 track appearances across 66 captured editions of the reviewed US Apple catalog, including single-track releases and the complete sequences of shared releases. A song can appear more than once. Dates identify the provider edition, not necessarily its first release. Physical releases, other territories, alternate profiles and unlinked collaborations may be missing.
