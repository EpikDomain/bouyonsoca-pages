Jeff Joseph’s captured listing mixes his own live and studio editions with guest rows on other artists’ releases. Compare the two 18-row live sequences, then use the performer column to find his rows on compilations and joint releases.

Compare the two live sequences

Jeff Joseph Live and Jeff Joseph and Gramacks New Generation (Live) each hold 18 rows across two discs, 10 then eight, and both open with Soucounyan. The second credits every row to Jeff Joseph & Gramacks New Generation and labels each title (Live), with spellings such as Wooy mi débar for Woy mi debar. Their provider dates are April 1, 2007 and April 29, 2011; matching titles do not establish the same recording.

Separate near-duplicate editions and later rights lines

More Fire / Zouka grande vitesse and More Fire (Zouka grande vitesse) list eight titles in the same order, with small differences such as Lougarou (Version anglaise) and Lougarou (English); one credits Jeff Joseph & Luc Léandry, the other mainly Luc Leandry & Jeff Joseph. Smiley’s Richess' (feat. Jeff Joseph) appears as a single and on More Love. Both carry ℗ 2026 rights lines beside 2011 and 2012 provider dates, so neither date is a recording date.
