Shadow’s included catalog has four editions: one 12-track collection, two Sweet Sweet Dreams editions and the D’Hardest single. Compare the Sweet Sweet Dreams sequences carefully, because the two editions differ in length and order.

Compare the two Sweet Sweet Dreams editions

Both Sweet Sweet Dreams editions display January 1, 1984. The seven-row edition opens with Let's Make It Up and closes with D'Hardest; the six-row edition opens with Way, Way Out and has no D'Hardest row. D'Hardest - Single pairs D'Hardest with D'Hardest (Version). Matching titles across these editions do not establish identical recordings.

Browse The Best of the Shadow Vol. 1 row by row

The Best of the Shadow Vol. 1 has 12 rows, opening with Bassman and ending with Christmas Nice, and displays June 28, 2009. It lists The Soca Boat and Stop the Boat as separate rows, and I Come Out to Play and I Come Out to Party as two distinct titles. Keep each row as listed rather than merging similar titles, and treat the date as a provider edition date.
