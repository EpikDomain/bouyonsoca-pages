Gordon Henderson’s captured listing combines albums under his name, shared Exile One releases and guest rows on other artists’ collections. Start with the album sequences, then use the performer column to find his rows on shared editions.

Start with the album sequences

Exile One 40 Anniversary, Vol. 1 runs 18 rows from Rosita to Interlude. Greetings From Martinique & Guatamala has 11, opening with Exile One Medley and including L'Hivernage and Ban Dang. On Kompa Time, credits move between Gordon Henderson, Georgie Jacquet and Philippe Dilo; the last row names only Philippe Dilo & Georgie Jacquet, so not every row is his.

Follow shared Exile One and guest editions

Old School Session: Green (Vert) is credited to Exile One featuring Gordon Henderson, and Vinyl Junkie Thangs lists Exile One’s Funky Crookie (feat. Gordon Henderson) as one of its 18 rows; both releases also appear in the Exile One catalog. With Zanma, he shares Sing Caribbean Christmas - Single and is featured on two of the five rows of Zanma ka chanté nwèl - EP.
