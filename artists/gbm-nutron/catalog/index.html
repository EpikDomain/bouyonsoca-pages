Move between full sequences and shared credits

Calypso: The Unsung Legacy contains 15 tracks, Carmen nine and Fuego eight in these editions. Ten has ten rows under GBM Milko and Gbmnutron: Practice and E.O.T.Y. are credited to Gbmnutron, Heart Attack to GBM Milko, and Life 101 to both. The edition credit therefore cannot be substituted for every track’s performer.
