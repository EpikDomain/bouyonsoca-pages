Preedy’s captured catalog is mostly single-track editions and shared riddim releases. Start with the five-track Maloney Story EP, then compare same-titled singles, labelled remixes and anthem editions whose performer lists differ.

Start with Maloney Story and its related singles

Maloney Story - EP opens with its title track and includes Pray (Acoustic Version) with Kimi and Changes with Stadic. Pray also has its own single credited to Preedy & Kimi. Changes appears on two separate single editions, both displaying July 30, 2019, with the same title, performers and duration; only their provider rights lines differ. Blaze Fire - Single holds two identically titled rows with no distinguishing label.

Keep remixes and anthem credits apart

Freaky Girl with Tommy Lee Sparta appears as a 2017 single, as a Remastered single that lists Preedy as featured, and as a 2018 Remix single with an instrumental row. Together (Digicel Anthem) has two single editions with the same date and different performer lists: one names Swappi, the other Nailah Blackman. Keep both rather than treating one as a duplicate.
