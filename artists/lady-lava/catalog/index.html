Lady Lava
Release catalog

Browse the captured releases, compare tracklists, and listen through the credited platform links.

Lady Lava’s inventory is especially useful for following short releases and their separately named versions. Ring Finger, In Deh and Bedtime Story offer different browsing tasks: finding the accompanying instrumental, comparing a radio edit, or locating a song alongside another title.

Read the version label before choosing

Ring Finger - Single contains Ring Finger credited to Lady Lava and Summer Steam Riddim (Instrumental) credited to Aaron Duncan. In Deh - Single lists In Deh, In Deh (Radio Edit) and One Man; Bedtime Story has a separate Radio Edit row. These labels identify what the provider displays, without resolving every recording or edit relationship.

Follow guest appearances without taking over the album

Pepper Vine has three entries: the unsuffixed title, Hindi Version and Steam Version, each retaining its displayed feature and performer wording. GYALGEBRA includes WHAT IS THE TIME (feat. Lady Lava) within a wider Major Lazer and Diplo release. Release Riddim also contains songs credited to Nelly Cottoy and Squid. Repeated identical titles stay separate edition rows unless stronger version evidence explains them.

What this inventory covers

This inventory contains 119 track appearances across 72 captured editions of the reviewed US Apple catalog, including single-track releases and the complete sequences of shared releases. A song can appear more than once. Dates identify the provider edition, not necessarily its first release. The lookup requested up to 200 collections; source shelves and their pagination limits remain visible in the coverage record. Physical releases, other territories, alternate profiles and unlinked collaborations may be missing. 2 alternate provider cohorts remain outside this inventory pending separate identity review.
