Jus Now
Release catalog

Browse the captured releases, compare tracklists, and listen through the credited platform links.

Jus Now’s catalog brings together EP sequences, named remix collections and shared riddim releases. Start with One Time or Cyah Help It for a short sequence, then use the edition and version labels to navigate Way Up and Jus Now: The Remixes.

Distinguish two Way Up editions

Both captured Way Up editions contain six tracks. Their fourth rows differ in the displayed title: Dun D Place (feat. Stylo G) on one and a Radio Edit suffix on the other. Jus Now: The Remixes separately lists named SNØW, Part2Style and Team Salut treatments of Dun D Place. Keep these observations distinct; shared titles do not prove an identical audio master.

Read the collective release row by row

Mosh Pit Riddim includes Fete Is Fete credited to Asa Bantan and Jus Now, alongside other performers and an instrumental. Kaminari Riddim has separate rows featuring Nailah Blackman, Pumpa and Mr Killa. Bombay Tea Riddim includes the three Pepper Vine-related entries and Rags. Its Hindi row is spelled Pepper (feat. Drupatee & Jus Now) [Hindi Version]; retain that source wording rather than silently correcting it.

A song can appear more than once. Dates identify the provider edition, not necessarily its first release. Physical releases, other territories, alternate profiles and unlinked collaborations may be missing.
