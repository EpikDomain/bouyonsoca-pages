This page covers the Lord Shorty provider listing: 10 captured editions with 89 track rows. Compare the two Sweet Music editions before choosing a track, and treat titles that recur on later-dated collections as separate rows, not the same recording.

Compare the Lord Shorty album editions

Two Sweet Music editions both display January 10, 1976, but one has 10 rows and the other eight; Cory Iron and Kim appear only in the longer one. Gone Gone Gone includes Indrani and a row titled This Song, and the later-dated Love in the Caribbean Lord Shorty and Friends lists This Song (feat. Ricky Gibson). The shared title does not establish the same recording.

Read the provider dates as edition labels

The Love Man displays January 12, 1974 and Endless Vibrations January 8, 1975, while Sokah Soul of Calypso opens with Sokah the Soul of Calypso. We Have Love - EP has six rows, the shortest sequence here, and lists its first row as We Hve Love. Lord Shorty Anthology One Calypso displays October 14, 2025. All of these are provider edition dates, not original-release dates.

What this source selection covers

The selected US provider cohort contains 89 track appearances across 10 captured editions, none of them single-track editions.
