Compare album editions and their labelled mixes

Under My Spell appears twice with 14 rows each. One edition displays November 2, 2001 and puts Road March third; the other displays May 28, 2002 and puts Doctor On Call third. Renaissance has 16 rows, pairing No More, Road Jam and Best On the Road with labelled remix or road-mix rows, and it lists Whole Day three ways. Carnival Please Stay, Vol. 1 (Live) is a separate six-track live EP.

Use instrumentals and riddim credits to navigate singles

Many two-row singles pair a vocal with its instrumental, as Truck Driver and Bacchanal Come with David Rudder do. Truck Driver also has a separate one-row single edition and a row on the album Shurwayne. On Bangrah Riddim, Shurwayne Winchester is credited only on Hottah Than Kuchela (feat. Ravi B); the other rows name Showtime and S.T.E.M.Z Productions.
