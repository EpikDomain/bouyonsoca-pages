Explainer’s captured catalog holds two albums under his name and seven multi-artist collections. Start with The Very Best of Explainer Vol.1 & 2 and Explosion, then use the performer column to locate his individual rows within the compilations.

Start with the two Explainer albums

The Very Best of Explainer Vol.1 & 2 has 17 rows, opening with Lorraine and including Rasta Chick and Ras Mas. Explosion has 10 and pairs Politicians at row six with Politicians (Dance Mix) at row 10. The two editions display June 1, 2008 and January 16, 2009; those are provider edition dates, not original-release dates.

Locate repeated titles across compilations

Lorraine also appears on Sweet Sweet Soca, while The Ultimate Soca Gold Collection lists Lorraine (Re-Make) [feat. Bunji Garlin]. Rasta Chick reappears on Golden Hitz of Calypso and Soca Vol.1, and Ras Mas on the 29-track Calypso Dreams - Soundtrack. Each compilation keeps every other performer’s rows, and a shared title does not establish the same recording.

Repeated titles remain separate observations. Shared releases retain all displayed performers. Edition dates are not necessarily first-release dates. The lookup requested up to 200 collections.
