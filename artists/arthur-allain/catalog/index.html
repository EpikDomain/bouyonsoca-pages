Arthur Allain’s captured listing includes two 10-track albums, a seven-track set and many singles and riddim rows. Compare album rows with their separate single editions, keeping each displayed performer string.

EvaLucianary and its singles

EvaLucianary has 10 rows and includes True Friends (feat. Hollywood HP), Slow Motion and We Liming (feat. Shemmy J & Sedale). Each of those titles also has its own single edition displaying June 16, 2019; the True Friends single credits Arthur Allain & Hollywood HP, and the We Liming single names Arthur Allain, Sedale & Shemmy J. Matching titles do not establish identical recordings.

Getting It Good and its shared rows

Getting It Good has 10 rows, including Flights No Feelings with Imran Nerdy and Praises Refix with Ricky T. Flights No Feelings also appears on Big Feast Riddim - EP, credited to Boogy Rankss, Arthur Allain & Imran Nerdy. Praises has separate Red Mask Riddim and Refix singles, the latter also naming Stixx.
