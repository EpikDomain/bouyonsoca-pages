Claudette Peters’s captured listing is mostly single-track editions, plus rows on soca compilations and a riddim EP. Shared singles keep every displayed performer, so follow the full credit string rather than the edition title alone.

Find her rows on compilations

D'Soca Zone: 4th Jump lists One Tump at row 17, and D'Soca Zone: The 7th Flag Up credits Flaunt It (feat. Claudette Peters) to Taxik. Soca758, Vol. 3 lists No Tomorrow at row four, and Soca Gold 2018 lists Feeling Good at row 17. Each compilation keeps every other performer’s rows, which belong to those performers.

Shared singles keep every credit

Go So is credited to Ricardo Drue & Claudette Peters, Its Carnival to Kisha Kay, Claudette Peters & Nicole David, and Can Do to Ricky T, Claudette Peters & Lavaman. Play De Bouyon and Me & U(Jamming Together) both name MrBenji alongside her. Keep the full performer string on each row.
