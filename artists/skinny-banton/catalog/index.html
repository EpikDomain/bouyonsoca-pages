Skinny Banton’s captured listing is mostly single-track editions, with shared riddims and compilation rows. Follow Soak It Good through its related titles, then separate similarly named jab singles.

Follow Soak It Good and related titles

Soak It Good - Single displays February 20, 2013 with a ℗ 2014 rights line. Salt Fish Remix (Soak It Good) [feat. Ricky T] is a separate single, Soca Gold 2015 lists Soak It at row six, and Lucian Bacchanal : The Best from Stratosphere Muzic 2015, Vol. 2 lists Saltfish (feat. Ricky T) at row four. Related titles do not establish identical recordings.

Separate similarly named jab titles

91 Riddim - Single pairs Skinny Banton’s Jab Behavior with Synnah’s Cocoa, while Jab Behaviour - Single is a separate 2016 edition with a different spelling. Dirty Dirty and Dirty Dirty (Doh Bother We) are separate 2015 singles. COALS DUST RIDDIM - EP features him on Heartbeat and Yard Fowl, with Temptress and Brother B on other rows.

What this source selection covers

Six further captured editions are excluded pending edition-specific identity evidence and are not counted here. Repeated titles remain separate observations. Shared releases retain all displayed performers. Edition dates are not necessarily first-release dates. The lookup requested up to 200 collections, and a browser collection extended the singles shelf. Physical releases, other territories and unlinked collaborations may be missing.
