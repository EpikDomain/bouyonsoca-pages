Behind the music

The story so far.

Patrice Roberts is a soca singer from Toco, Trinidad and Tobago, whose musical path began in the island's calypso competition culture. Her work with Machel Montano includes Band of the Year, and her solo catalogue has developed a voice and perspective of its own. Interviews describe an artist increasingly confident about expressing her experiences as a woman and performer through her music. Mind My Business places a direct, memorable message over the Happy Papi Riddim. Tender, produced by AdvoKit Productions, explores a softer, romantic setting. Heard together, the two tracks demonstrate the range within her soca repertoire: clear personality, melodic phrasing and rhythmic ease, whether the song calls for collective release or a more intimate mood.
