Read See Mi Yah by disc and track

See Mi Yah: The Best of Benjai places Fun Fun Fun at disc one, track one and Wine Wine at disc two, track one. Each disc has 15 rows, so the restart is not a duplicate to remove. Some of its titles also surface on compilations: Over & Over on Soca Gold 2002 and Drunk Again on Soca758, Vol. 2. Treat each as a separate edition appearance, not as proof of the same recording.

Separate riddim rows and repeated singles

Peace Time Riddim credits Even If to Benjai, Things Could Be Better to Kango and the instrumental to Mr. Roots, so only one row is his. Carnival Friend, credited to Kes & Benjai, has two single editions displaying November 19, 2024 and November 19, 2025. The same title also opens the five-track Blue Phone Riddim EP, whose other rows name other performers.
