Start with the two longer sequences

The Art of Flex contains 11 tracks and begins with Lime Light (feat. Konshens), No Hands and Doh Wah Love. Little Miss Arima contains 13 tracks; its opening sequence includes Music Is Life credited to Nessa Preppy, M1 aka Menace and Dj Spider, and Make It Clap credited to Nessa Preppy and Lady Lava. These edition credits do not establish a debut-album order.

Compare a labelled version or compilation appearance

Doh Wah Love - Single contains an unsuffixed row and Doh Wah Love (Raw). Hot Gyal has a separate Radio Edit entry. Issa Snack appears on Do This Riddim and Soca Gold 2019 with Nessa Preppy and Travis World named in the performer strings. Those are traceable edition appearances, not proof that the underlying audio is the same master.
