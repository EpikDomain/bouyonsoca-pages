Behind the music

The story so far.

Machel Montano has built a career around carrying Trinidad and Tobago's soca music to wider audiences. He began performing as a child, formed a band while still at school and released Too Young To Soca early in his recording career. His music connects the spectacle of Carnival with studio collaborations and international live performance. Like Ah Boss is a direct route into his commanding, high-energy style; Fast Wine gives listeners another side of a catalogue that moves between forceful road music and more melodic dance records. Collaborations with artists including Super Blue, Bunji Garlin and Skinny Fabulous place him within a broad Caribbean musical network. His official biography describes a continuing artistic ambition to expand soca's audience while remaining connected to its home culture.
