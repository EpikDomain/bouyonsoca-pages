The music deserves a little more context.

A great song can reach you from anywhere. Knowing who made it, where they come from and which scene they belong to makes the discovery richer. That's the idea behind BouyonSoca.

Our opening collection brings together 79 artists and groups across bouyon, soca, zouk, calypso, jazz, steelpan, caribbean, creole, dancehall, cadence-lypso, reggae, dennery segment, trinibad, chutney soca, shatta, rap, and jamoo. This is a starting point, not a claim to represent every sound in the Caribbean. We keep the roots visible and give every artist a place to tell their story.

Sources before superlatives.

Profiles are written from artist biographies, interviews, official music catalogs and cultural sources. You can open the source list on each artist and genre page. Our listening picks are editorial selections, not streaming charts or popularity rankings.

Artist profiles distinguish individual performers, bands and musical relationships. Photographs and original typographic artist cards are labeled and credited. We do not claim an artist endorses BouyonSoca simply because they appear in the guide.

The music belongs to the artists.

Listening links open the recording on its source platform, such as YouTube, Spotify or Apple Music. We do not rehost audio. Availability, ads, sign-in requirements and playback features depend on the service and your region.

Use the artist directory to search a name or island. Filter the collection by genre, or try “Surprise me” to discover someone new.
